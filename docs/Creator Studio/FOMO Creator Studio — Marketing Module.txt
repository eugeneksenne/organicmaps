# FOMO Creator Studio — Marketing Module

## Product Vision

The **Marketing Module** is the growth engine of FOMO Creator Studio.

Its purpose is to help venues, event organisers, DJs, and artists reach the right audience, increase attendance, sell more tickets, drive venue visits, and grow their brands.

This is **not** a social media scheduler.

It is a **nightlife marketing platform** built specifically for promoting nightlife experiences inside the FOMO ecosystem and, where supported, across connected channels.

---

# Design Principles

The Marketing Module should be:

* Goal-driven, with campaigns tied to measurable business outcomes.
* Integrated with Events, Ticketing, Flash Drops, and Audience.
* Performance-focused, emphasizing ROI rather than vanity metrics.
* Simple enough for a club owner, powerful enough for a festival organiser.
* Mobile-first, enabling campaign management from anywhere.

# Guiding Principle

The Marketing Module should empower nightlife professionals to **grow their business, not just advertise**. Every campaign should be connected to a measurable business outcome—whether that's filling a venue, selling tickets, increasing Flash Drop participation, or building a loyal audience. By integrating seamlessly with Events, Ticketing, Audience, and Intelligence, marketing becomes an ongoing growth strategy rather than a collection of disconnected promotional tools.
