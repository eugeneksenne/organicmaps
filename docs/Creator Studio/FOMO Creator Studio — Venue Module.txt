# FOMO Creator Studio — Venue Module

## Product Vision

The **Venue Module** is the digital headquarters for every verified nightlife venue on FOMO.

It enables venue owners and managers to operate, maintain, and grow their venue from a single workspace. Beyond simply editing a profile, the Venue Module acts as the venue's **digital twin**—its public identity, operational dashboard, and collaboration hub.

Whether managing a nightclub, rooftop bar, lounge, live music venue, or entertainment space, creators can control how their venue appears on FOMO, coordinate staff, host events, launch promotions, and analyze venue performance.

---

# Design Principles

The Venue Module should be:

* Operational, supporting day-to-day venue management.
* Business-focused, helping venues grow attendance and revenue.
* Collaborative, allowing multiple staff members with role-based permissions.
* Always live, reflecting the venue's current status and upcoming experiences.
* Integrated with Events, Flash Drops, Ticketing, Marketing, and Audience.

# Guiding Principle

The Venue Module should feel like **running a venue, not editing a profile**. It is the operational command center for a nightlife business, connecting venue identity, live operations, staff management, events, Flash Drops, guest feedback, and business intelligence into one cohesive workspace. Every feature should help venue owners deliver better nightlife experiences while growing their business and strengthening their presence within the FOMO ecosystem.
