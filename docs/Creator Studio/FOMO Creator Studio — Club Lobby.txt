# FOMO Club Lobby

## Product Vision

The **Club Lobby** is the digital front door of every nightlife venue on FOMO.

It is not a social feed, review page, or venue directory.

Its purpose is to answer one question:

> **"Should I go here tonight?"**

Every section should build trust, create excitement, reduce uncertainty, and help users confidently decide to visit the venue.

The Club Lobby combines trusted venue information, live nightlife experiences, and operational features into a single immersive experience without becoming a social network.

---

# Design Principles

The Club Lobby should be:

* Trust-first
* Live and dynamic
* Emotionally persuasive
* Visually immersive
* Mobile-first
* Conversion-focused
* Privacy-conscious
* Easy to explore

# 1. Hero Experience

The hero section is the emotional entry point into the venue.

Instead of a static image, it cycles through premium media such as:

* Venue cover video
* Highlight reel
* Live Broadcast preview
* Featured Moment

The transition is automatic and subtle.

Example:

```text

🎥 HERO EXPERIENCE

❤️ Save

FOMO Club ✓

🟢 Open Now

Closes 04:00

⭐ 4.7 (2.4k)

📍 Rosebank

1.2 km away

[ View Live ]

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

The hero immediately communicates the venue's atmosphere.

# 3. Live Updates

A live feed of trusted updates that disappear after 24 hours.

* Venue
* DJs
* Music Artists
* Event Organisers
* Verified Attendees (if enabled)

* DJ soundcheck
* VIP tables available
* Champagne show starting
* Queue update
* Birthday celebration

This is operational, not social.

# 9. Moments

Moments are permanent, curated collections created by the venue.

Unlike Tonight at the Venue, Moments never expire.

They showcase the venue's best experiences.

Examples:

* New Year's Eve
* Black Coffee Live
* Anniversary Celebration
* Celebrity Nights
* Festival Takeovers

Each Moment contains:

* Photos
* Videos
* Event information
* Highlights
* Performers

Example:

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

# 18. Sticky Action Dock

Always visible at the bottom.

```text
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📍 follow

♡ Save

📍 Route

✨ Plan Night

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```
### follow

follows the venue for updates.

### Save

Adds the venue to favorites.

### Route

Opens navigation immediately.

### Plan Night

Starts a nightlife plan.

Options:

* Solo Night
* With Friends
* Group Night
* Add to My Moves

* Hero media
* Live Updates

* Venue Highlights
* Amenities
* Venue Notices
* Venue Information
* Live Broadcast

# AI Features (Future Roadmap)

AI can assist venues by:

* Recommending hero media.
* Suggesting Moments to feature.
* Detecting outdated venue information.
* Highlighting missing amenities.
* Recommending the best Flash Drops based on venue history.
* Suggesting optimal times to start Live Broadcasts.

AI supports content curation but does not publish changes automatically.

---

# Success Metrics

The Club Lobby should help FOMO:

* Increase venue visits.
* Increase event attendance.
* Increase Flash Drop redemption at venues.
* Improve route conversions.
* Increase follower growth for venues.
* Build trust through verified information.
* Showcase each venue's unique identity.

---

# Guiding Principle

The **Club Lobby** should feel like stepping through the digital front door of a venue. It combines trusted information, live experiences, curated memories, and real-time operational updates into a single immersive destination that helps users answer one simple question: **"Is this where I want to spend my night?"** By balancing emotional storytelling with verified data, the Club Lobby becomes a powerful conversion engine that inspires confidence, excitement, and real-world venue visits.
