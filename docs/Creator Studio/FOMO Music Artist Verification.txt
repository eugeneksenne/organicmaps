I think artists should be verified differently from venues because you're verifying **professional identity**, not ownership of a business.

The best approach is to treat this as **Professional Identity Verification**.

---

# FOMO Music Artist Verification

## Goal

Answer one question:

> **Is this person the official artist they claim to be?**

Not:

> "Are they famous?"

An independent artist with 500 monthly listeners should be just as eligible as one with 5 million, provided they control their official artist identity.

---

# Verification Methods

Creators can verify through one or more trusted professional platforms.

## Method 1 — Spotify for Artists ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Recommended)

This should be the primary method.

The artist signs in with their **Spotify for Artists** account.

FOMO verifies that they have access to the official artist profile.

Example:

```text
Continue with Spotify

↓

Spotify Login

↓

Official Artist

DJ Nova

✓ Verified
```

If successful:

* Verified Music Artist role is granted.
* Artist profile can be imported.
* Albums, genres, profile image, and links can be synced (subject to API availability and user consent).

This is the strongest signal because Spotify for Artists is designed for artist identity management.

---

## Method 2 — Facebook

The artist signs in with Facebook.

Instead of just matching an email address, FOMO should verify access to an **official professional presence**, such as:

* An official Artist Page.
* A Musician/Band Page.
* A verified professional Page (where applicable).

This proves they control the artist's public presence.

---

## Method 3 — YouTube Official Artist Channel

The artist signs into the Google account managing their Official Artist Channel.

This is another strong professional identity signal.

---

## Method 4 — Apple Music for Artists

If Apple provides access to artist management authentication, this is another excellent verification method.

---

## Method 5 — SoundCloud

For independent artists who actively manage their SoundCloud artist profile.

This is a useful secondary signal.

---

## Method 6 — Manual Review

Fallback for artists who don't use supported platforms.

Evidence may include:

* Official website.
* Label confirmation.
* Published releases.
* Performance contracts.
* Festival lineups.
* Press coverage.

---

# Import Artist Profile

After verification, FOMO can ask:

> **Import your professional profile?**

Imported fields might include:

* Artist name.
* Profile image.
* Genres.
* Biography.
* Social links.

The creator can review and edit imported information before publishing.

The DJ flow is almost identical, but the preferred professional platforms differ slightly.

Recommended order:

* Spotify (if they release music).
* SoundCloud.
* Beatport.
* Mixcloud.
* Facebook.

---

# One Important Distinction

There's a difference between **logging in with a platform** and **verifying an artist account**.

For example:

❌ This is **not enough**:

> Login with Facebook

Anyone can have a Facebook account.

✅ Better:

> Login with Facebook and verify you manage the official Artist Page.

Likewise:

❌ Logging into a regular Spotify listener account should not verify someone.

✅ Logging into **Spotify for Artists** (or otherwise proving management of the official artist profile) is the appropriate signal.

The same principle applies to every platform: FOMO should verify **control of the professional artist identity**, not merely ownership of a personal login.

## Recommended Verification Hierarchy

For Music Artists:

1. **Spotify for Artists** (primary).
2. **Apple Music for Artists**.
3. **YouTube Official Artist Channel**.
4. **Facebook Artist/Musician Page**.
5. **SoundCloud Artist profile**.
6. **Manual verification**.

For DJs:

1. **Beatport Artist** (where applicable).
2. **Spotify for Artists**.
3. **Mixcloud Pro/Creator**.
4. **SoundCloud Artist**.
5. **Facebook DJ Page**.
6. **Manual verification**.

This approach keeps verification focused on **professional identity**, just as venue claiming focuses on **business ownership**, ensuring Creator Studio remains a trusted platform for the people who genuinely create nightlife experiences.
