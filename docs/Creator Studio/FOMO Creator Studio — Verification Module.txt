# FOMO Creator Studio — Verification Module

## Product Vision

The **Verification Module** is the trust and identity system of FOMO Creator Studio.

Its purpose is to verify that every creator, venue, organiser, DJ, and music artist on the platform is authentic.

Verification protects users, builds confidence between creators, prevents fraud, and ensures that only legitimate nightlife professionals gain access to Creator Studio.

Verification is **not** a badge that can be purchased.

It is a professional validation process based on role-specific requirements.

---

# Design Principles

The Verification Module should be:

* Trust-first.
* Role-specific.
* Transparent.
* Secure.
* Easy to understand.
* Difficult to abuse.
* Fully auditable.

---

# Verification Goals

The module should:

* Verify creator identity.
* Verify businesses.
* Verify venue ownership.
* Verify professional DJs.
* Verify professional artists.
* Verify event organisers.
* Prevent impersonation.
* Build trust across the nightlife ecosystem.

---

# Venue Owner Verification

Purpose

Verify legitimate ownership or management of a nightlife venue.

Required evidence may include:

Business

* Business registration certificate
* Company registration number
* Tax registration (where applicable)

Venue

* Liquor license (where required)
* Municipal operating permit
* Lease agreement or ownership documents
* Utility bill linked to venue

Role Confirmation

* Proof of employment
* Director information
* Management authorization

Verification outcome

```text
Verified Venue Owner
```

Creators can then claim and manage their venue.

---

# Suspension

Verification may be suspended if:

* Fraud is detected.
* Business ownership changes.
* False documentation is submitted.
* Platform policies are violated.

Suspended creators lose Creator Studio access until issues are resolved.

---

# Security

Verification data is highly sensitive.

Security requirements

* Encrypted document storage
* Secure transmission
* Role-based access
* Audit logging
* Data retention policies
* Compliance with privacy regulations

Documents should only be accessible to authorized verification staff.

---

# AI Verification Assistant (Future Roadmap)

The module can assist creators before submission.

Examples

* Detect blurry document uploads.
* Identify missing information.
* Suggest additional supporting evidence.
* Check document completeness.
* Warn about expired documents.

AI assists with preparation but does not make approval decisions.

# Guiding Principle

The Verification Module is the **foundation of trust** within the FOMO ecosystem. Every verified badge should represent a real venue, a legitimate organiser, a professional DJ, or an authentic music artist. By combining identity verification, role-specific evidence, secure document management, and transparent review workflows, the module ensures that Creator Studio remains an exclusive, professional environment where nightlife businesses and creators can confidently collaborate and grow.
