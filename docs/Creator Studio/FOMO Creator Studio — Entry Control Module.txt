I actually think the **Ticket Scanner** should not be treated as just a QR scanner.

It should be the **Entry Control Module**.

Nightlife venues don't just scan tickets—they manage the entire flow of people entering the venue. This module should handle:

* Online tickets
* Door sales
* Guest lists
* VIP access
* Staff entry
* Re-entry
* Capacity management
* Live occupancy

This makes it valuable even for venues that don't sell online tickets through FOMO.

---

# FOMO Creator Studio — Entry Control Module

## Product Vision

The **Entry Control Module** is the operational hub used by venue staff to manage guest entry during events.

It enables secure ticket validation, guest check-ins, door sales, complimentary entry, capacity monitoring, and live occupancy tracking from a single mobile device.

It is designed for speed, reliability, and operation in busy nightlife environments.

---

# Design Principles

The module should be:

* One-handed operation
* Fast (<1 second per scan)
* Offline-capable
* Fraud-resistant
* Simple for security staff
* Real-time synchronized
* Multi-device compatible

---

# Supported Entry Types

The scanner supports:

* QR Tickets
* Door Sales
* Guest Lists
* VIP Lists
* Complimentary Tickets
* Staff Passes
* Artist Passes
* Media Passes
* Re-entry Passes

Every entry follows the same validation workflow.

```text

Will sync automatically.
```

# Fraud Detection

The scanner detects:

* Duplicate scans
* Screenshots of already-used tickets
* Wrong event
* Wrong date
* Cancelled tickets
* Refunded tickets
* Expired tickets

All incidents are logged.

# Staff Roles

Permissions are role-based.

## Door Staff

* Scan tickets
* Sell door entry
* Check in guests

---

## Supervisor

Everything Door Staff can do, plus:

* Override entry decisions
* Process manual check-ins
* View occupancy
* Manage re-entry

---

## Venue Manager

Full access.

Can:

* View reports
* Manage staff
* Close entry
* Export attendance
* Resolve disputes

---

# Security Features

Every action is audited.

Logs include:

* Staff member
* Device
* Time
* Entry type
* GPS (optional)
* Override reason

This protects venues against fraud and disputes.

# Integration with Creator Studio

The Entry Control Module integrates with:

* **Events** – active event selection and status.
* **Ticketing** – validates online tickets and records door sales.
* **Venue** – enforces venue capacity.
* **Analytics** – tracks attendance, peak entry times, and entry sources.
* **Flash Drops** – can verify eligibility for venue-redemption rewards.
* **Intelligence** – identifies bottlenecks, predicts peak arrival periods, and recommends staffing levels.

# Success Metrics

The Entry Control Module should help creators:

* Admit guests in under one second.
* Eliminate ticket fraud.
* Track real-time occupancy.
* Sell and record door tickets.
* Manage guest lists and VIP access.
* Operate reliably even with poor connectivity.

---

# Guiding Principle

The **Entry Control Module** should feel like the digital front gate of the venue. Whether a guest arrives with a FOMO ticket, buys entry at the door, joins a guest list, or enters through a VIP entrance, every interaction is managed through one fast, secure, and intelligent system. This turns a simple ticket scanner into a complete venue entry management platform that supports the entire nightlife operation.
