# FOMO Creator Studio — Profile Module

## Product Vision

The **Profile Module** is the professional identity hub of FOMO Creator Studio.

It represents the verified public identity of every creator on the platform—whether a venue owner, event organiser, DJ, or music artist.

Unlike a personal social media profile, a Creator Profile is a **professional portfolio** that establishes trust, showcases experience, and enables collaboration across the nightlife ecosystem.

Every verified creator has one profile, but may hold multiple professional roles.

---

# Design Principles

The Profile Module should be:

* Professional rather than social.
* Verification-first to establish authenticity.
* Portfolio-driven, showcasing work and achievements.
* Collaborative, enabling bookings and partnerships.
* Flexible enough to support multiple creator roles.
* Integrated with the entire FOMO ecosystem.

# Guiding Principle

The Profile Module should function as a creator's **professional identity and portfolio**, not as another social media page. Every section should strengthen trust, showcase expertise, and make it easier for venues, organisers, DJs, and artists to discover, evaluate, and collaborate with one another. By integrating seamlessly with Events, Marketing, Collaboration, and Verification, the profile becomes the creator's long-term professional presence within the FOMO ecosystem.
