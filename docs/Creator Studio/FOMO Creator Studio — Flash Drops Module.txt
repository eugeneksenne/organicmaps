# FOMO Creator Studio — Flash Drops Module

## Product Vision

The **Flash Drops Module** is FOMO's signature engagement and venue activation system.

Flash Drops are **limited-time, limited-quantity real-world rewards** designed to drive people to nightlife venues, increase attendance, reward loyal guests, and create moments of excitement.

Unlike coupons or digital vouchers, Flash Drops are **not redeemed in the app**.

They are discovered through FOMO and **redeemed physically at participating venues** by venue staff.

Flash Drops can be attached to an event or created as standalone venue campaigns.

---

# Design Principles

The Flash Drops Module should be:

* Real-world first, encouraging physical venue visits.
* Time-sensitive to create urgency (FOMO).
* Simple to launch in minutes.
* Flexible enough for events and everyday venue promotions.
* Measurable with clear performance analytics.
* Secure against abuse and fraud.

# Redemption Process

Flash Drops are redeemed **only at the venue**.

Workflow:

```text
User discovers Flash Drop

↓

Visits venue

↓

Shows reward to staff

↓

Staff verifies eligibility

↓

Staff confirms redemption

↓

Reward issued
```

No digital coupon is consumed automatically in the app.

---

# Fraud Prevention

To protect creators and venues:

* Redemptions require staff confirmation.
* Inventory updates instantly.
* Duplicate redemptions are prevented.
* Redemption history is recorded.
* Campaigns can be paused instantly.

Future enhancements:

* QR-assisted verification
* Device validation
* Staff PIN confirmation
* Offline redemption syncing

# Guiding Principle

Flash Drops are **not digital coupons**—they are **real-world experiences** that motivate people to leave the couch, visit a venue, and participate in nightlife. The module should make it effortless for venues and organisers to launch compelling, time-sensitive rewards while giving them clear insight into how those campaigns influence attendance, engagement, and business growth. By connecting Flash Drops with Events, Marketing, Audience, and Intelligence, they become a core part of FOMO's unique value proposition rather than just another promotional feature.
