# FOMO Creator Studio — Events Module

## Product Vision

The **Events Module** is the operational heart of FOMO Creator Studio.

Every nightlife experience begins with an event.

Whether it's a nightclub party, rooftop session, festival, DJ performance, live concert, themed experience, or private nightlife event, the Events Module provides a complete workspace to **plan, collaborate, publish, operate, and analyze** that experience.

This is **not** an event creation form.

It is the complete operational workspace for an event from the first idea until the last guest leaves the venue.

---

# Design Principles

The Events Module should be:

* Workspace-driven, not form-driven.
* Collaborative, allowing multiple verified creators to work together.
* Modular, with optional features like ticketing and Flash Drops.
* Operational, supporting creators before, during, and after the event.
* Mobile-first, enabling management on the go.
* Data-driven, providing actionable insights and analytics.

# Guiding Principle

The Events Module should feel like **building and operating a live nightlife experience**, not filling in forms. Every aspect of an event—from planning, ticketing, Flash Drops, and performer management to guest operations, promotion, collaboration, and post-event analytics—should exist within a single, cohesive workspace that becomes the central hub for everyone involved in making the event a success.
