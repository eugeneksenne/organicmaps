# FOMO Creator Studio — Event Organiser Verification Module

## Product Vision

The **Event Organiser Verification Module** verifies legitimate event organisers and event management companies before granting access to the Event Organiser role within FOMO Creator Studio.

Unlike Venue Owners, who verify ownership of a physical venue, Event Organisers verify their **professional business identity**.

The primary verification method is through a **business email with a custom domain**, ensuring that organisers represent a legitimate organization rather than a personal account.

Once verified, organisers gain access to create and manage events, ticketing, marketing campaigns, Flash Drops, and collaborate with venues, DJs, and artists.

---

The Event Organiser Verification Module should be:

* Fast to complete.

# Verification Workflow

```text
Select Role

↓

Business Email

↓

Email Validation

↓

Send OTP

↓

Verify OTP

↓

Organization Detection

↓

Complete Organization Profile

↓

Approved

↓

Creator Studio Access
```

---

# Step 1 — Select Creator Role

```text
I am a...

○ Venue Owner

○ Event Organiser

○ DJ

○ Music Artist
```

↓

Event Organiser

---

# Step 2 — Business Email

The organiser enters their business email.

Examples:

```text
events@redbull.com

bookings@ultra.co.za

info@festivalafrica.com
```

Not accepted for automatic verification:

```text
party@gmail.com

events@yahoo.com

club@hotmail.com
```

---

# Business Email Requirements

The email must:

* Use a custom business domain.
* Be able to receive email.
* Belong to the organization.
* Not be from a free email provider.

---

# Step 3 — Email Validation

FOMO validates:

* Email format
* Custom domain
* Domain MX records
* Existing verified organization (if applicable)

If valid:

```text
✓ Business Email Detected

Domain

redbull.com
```

---

# Step 4 — Send Verification Code

The organiser confirms:

```text
Send verification code to

events@redbull.com

[ Send Code ]
```

FOMO sends a secure one-time password (OTP) to the email address.

---

# Step 5 — Verify Email

The organiser enters the verification code.

```text
Verification Code

_ _ _ _ _ _
```

↓

Correct code

↓

```text
✓ Email Verified
```

The verified business email becomes the trusted identity for the organization.

---

# Step 6 — Organization Detection

FOMO extracts the domain.

Example:

```text
events@redbull.com

↓

redbull.com
```

If the organization already exists:

```text
Organization Found

Red Bull

✓ Verified Organization
```

The organiser confirms the organization before continuing.

If the domain is new:

```text
Organization Name

____________________
```

The organiser enters the organization name, which becomes the organization's profile in FOMO.

---

* Logo

* Description
* Website

* Country
* Social Links

This profile is used across Creator Studio and public event pages.

# Guiding Principle

The Event Organiser Verification Module should establish **business identity**, not just individual identity. By using verified custom-domain email addresses as the primary trust signal, FOMO creates a fast, low-friction onboarding experience for professional event organizations while maintaining a high standard of authenticity. Once verified, the organization becomes a collaborative workspace capable of managing events, tickets, marketing, Flash Drops, and teams within the FOMO nightlife ecosystem.
