I actually think a **wizard is the best UX** for FOMO.

Creating a nightlife event involves a lot of information, and putting everything into one long form is overwhelming. A wizard also lets you validate each step, auto-save progress, and adapt the flow based on choices (for example, only showing ticket options if the event is ticketed).

I would design it like this:

# FOMO Creator Studio — Event Creation Wizard

## Design Principles

The wizard should be:

* Fast (3–5 minutes to complete)
* Auto-saving after every step
* Mobile-first
* Smart (pre-fill known data)
* Flexible (skip irrelevant steps)
* Visually driven

Every step should end with a **Continue** button and allow **Save as Draft**.

## Step 5 — Entry & Ticketing

### Purpose

Configure how guests will access the event.

FOMO supports:

* Free entry
* Door sales only
* Online ticket sales
* Hybrid (online + door sales)

This gives creators maximum flexibility while allowing FOMO to facilitate online sales when desired.

---

# Step 1 — Choose Entry Method

```text
How will guests enter this event?

○ Free Event

○ Door Sales Only

○ Online Tickets

○ Online Tickets + Door Sales (Recommended)
```

---

## Free Event

Guests do not need to purchase tickets.

Optional:

* Free RSVP
* Capacity limit
* Guest registration

FOMO creates a free guest list instead of tickets.

---

## Door Sales Only

Perfect for clubs that sell tickets at the entrance.

Creators configure:

* Door Entry Price
* Opening Time
* Closing Time
* Accepted Payment Methods
* Door Capacity (optional)

Example:

```text
Door Entry

Price

R200

Sales

At the venue only

Capacity

500 Guests
```

No online ticket sales are enabled.

---

## Online Tickets

Tickets are sold exclusively through the FOMO app.

Users purchase tickets before arriving.

Creators configure ticket types.

---

## Online Tickets + Door Sales

The most flexible option.

Example:

```text
------------------
```

This encourages early purchases while still accommodating walk-ins.

# Integration with Other Modules

This step connects directly with:

* **Event Management** – displays ticket sales and capacity.
* **Ticket Purchase** – enables in-app purchases for online tickets.
* **Door Scanner** – validates QR tickets and records door entries.
* **Analytics** – tracks online sales versus door sales.
* **Promote** – allows creators to promote ticket sales.
* **Intelligence** – recommends pricing, capacity, and sales strategies.

---

# Future Enhancement: Dynamic Pricing

For creators who opt in, FOMO could offer **Dynamic Pricing**.

Examples:

* Increase prices automatically as ticket tiers sell out.
* Raise door prices when the venue approaches capacity.
* Launch last-minute discounts during slow sales.
* Trigger special offers based on weather or local demand.

This keeps the default experience simple while giving advanced organisers tools to maximize revenue without manually updating prices.

# Smart Features

The wizard should intelligently adapt:

* Skip the Tickets step for free events.
* Skip the Venue step if the event was started from a specific venue.
* Pre-fill recurring event details.
* Remember commonly used dress codes and age restrictions.
* Suggest previous lineups for recurring events.

Instead of treating the wizard as a **form**, make it a **production workflow**.

Each completed step unlocks the next stage of preparing the event:

```

        ✓
```

This feels like producing a real nightlife experience rather than filling out administrative fields. It's more aligned with how venue owners and event organisers think about their work, and it naturally integrates the rest of Creator Studio—Ticketing, Flash Drops, Marketing, and Collaboration—into a single guided experience.
