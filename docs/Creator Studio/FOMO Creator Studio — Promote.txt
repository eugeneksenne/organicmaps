This is probably the **most important revenue module** in the entire FOMO ecosystem.

I would actually avoid calling it **Marketing** internally. That's a generic business term used by every dashboard.

For FOMO, this module should feel like **growing nightlife**, not just running ads.

I would rename it:

# 🚀 FOMO Creator Studio — Promote

or

# 🚀 Promote

It's shorter, action-oriented, and users immediately understand what it's for.

---

# Product Vision

The **Promote Module** is FOMO's advertising and growth platform.

It enables venues, event organisers, DJs, and artists to increase visibility, attract more attendees, sell more tickets, and drive more venue visits through intelligent promotional campaigns.

Unlike traditional advertising platforms, every promotion is designed specifically for nightlife.

The objective is simple:

> **Help creators fill venues, sell tickets, and grow audiences while generating advertising revenue for FOMO.**

# How FOMO Makes Money

This module becomes one of several revenue engines:

1. **Sponsored Campaigns** – creators pay to promote venues, events, tickets, DJs, artists, Club Lobbies, and Flash Drops.
2. **Featured Placements** – premium positioning in Discover, Search, and Nearby.
3. **Push Notification Campaigns** – paid delivery of timely event alerts.
4. **Live Broadcast Boosts** – increase exposure for live streams.
5. **Premium Creator Plans** – unlock advanced promotion tools and AI optimization.

This creates recurring revenue while directly helping creators grow their nightlife businesses.

# One Feature I Think Could Differentiate FOMO

Instead of a traditional ad manager, introduce **Night Goals**.

When a creator taps **Promote**, they don't start by building an ad. They start by choosing a goal:

* Fill my venue tonight.
* Sell 100 more tickets.
* Get more people to watch the live broadcast.
* Bring people in before 10 PM.
* Increase Flash Drop redemptions.
* Grow my followers.

FOMO's AI then builds the campaign automatically—choosing the audience, placement, budget allocation, and timing based on historical nightlife performance. The creator reviews the plan, adjusts if needed, and launches it with a single tap.

This shifts the experience from **campaign management** to **business outcomes**, making the module far more accessible while creating a compelling reason for creators to spend money on the platform.
