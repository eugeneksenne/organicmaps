# FOMO Creator Studio — Venue Claiming Module

## Product Vision

The **Venue Claiming Module** allows verified Venue Owners to securely claim ownership or management of nightlife venues that already exist on FOMO.

Unlike traditional business directories where owners manually create listings, **FOMO automatically builds its venue database using Google Places and other trusted location data sources**. This means that in most cases, the venue already exists before the owner joins FOMO.

The purpose of Venue Claiming is to connect a verified creator with the correct venue through **ownership intelligence**, **business identity verification**, and **secure authentication**.

Once a venue is claimed, it becomes the owner's operational headquarters within Creator Studio, unlocking Venue Management, Events, Ticketing, Flash Drops, Marketing, Team Management, and Intelligence.

---

# Design Principles

The Venue Claiming Module should be:

* Fast for legitimate owners.
* Secure against fraudulent claims.
* Privacy-first.
* AI-assisted.
* Verification-driven.
* Auditable.
* Scalable worldwide.

# Who Can Claim a Venue?

If a creator has not completed Venue Owner Verification, they are redirected to the Verification Module first.

* Logged-in email

---

# Method 1 — Google Account Match ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

If the Google Places listing contains:

```text
clubxyz@gmail.com
```

and the creator is logged into FOMO using:

```text
clubxyz@gmail.com
```

FOMO detects:

```text
✓ Google Account Matches
Business Email
```

No OTP is required because the authenticated Google account already proves control of the email.

This is the fastest verification method.

---

# Method 2 — Business Email OTP ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

FOMO retrieves the business email from the venue record.

Privacy is always protected.

Example:

```text
Business Email

clu*****@gmail.com

[ Send Code ]
```

A six-digit OTP is sent.

Correct code:

```text
✓ Business Email Verified
```

---

# Method 3 — SMS Verification ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Business phone:

```text
07******07

[ Send Code ]
```

OTP verification confirms ownership.

---

# Method 4 — WhatsApp Verification ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

If the business number supports WhatsApp:

```text
Business WhatsApp

07******07

[ Send Code ]
```

The owner receives a WhatsApp verification code.

# Method 5 — Manual Verification

If business contacts are no longer accessible:

```text

```

Required evidence may include:

* Business registration
* Liquor license

* Utility bill
* Authorization letter
* Proof of ownership

Manual review is performed by FOMO.

# Guiding Principle

The Venue Claiming Module should feel less like a traditional verification form and more like **claiming a digital business already waiting for you**. By combining Google Places data, authenticated business identities, OTP verification, and an Ownership Intelligence Engine, FOMO can provide a near-instant claiming experience for legitimate owners while maintaining the security, transparency, and trust required to operate a global nightlife platform.
