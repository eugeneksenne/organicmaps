# FOMO Ticket Purchase Experience

## Product Vision

The **Ticket Purchase Experience** enables users to discover, purchase, store, transfer, and redeem tickets directly within the FOMO app.

It provides a secure, fast, and mobile-first checkout experience while giving creators complete control over ticket sales.

The goal is to make buying a ticket take less than a minute.

---

# Design Principles

The experience should be:

* Fast (under 60 seconds)
* Secure
* Transparent
* Mobile-first
* QR-code based
* Fraud-resistant
* Offline-capable for venue scanning

---

# 1. Event Ticket Section

On every ticketed event, users see a dedicated ticket section.

Example:

```text
Tickets

Early Bird
R150
Available

General Admission
R250

VIP
R500

[ Buy Tickets ]
```

Each ticket type displays:

* Price
* Availability
* Benefits
* Remaining quantity (optional)
* Sale end date

---

# 2. Ticket Selection

Users choose:

* Ticket type
* Quantity

Example:

```text
General Admission

[-] 2 [+]

R250 each

Total

R500
```

---

# 3. Checkout

The checkout screen summarizes:

* Event
* Venue
* Date
* Selected tickets
* Fees
* Total amount

Users confirm before paying.

---

# 4. Payments

Support regional and international payment methods.

Examples:

* Credit/Debit Cards
* Apple Pay
* Google Pay
* EFT/Open Banking (where available)
* Local wallets and payment providers

Payment providers can vary by country while the FOMO experience remains the same.

---

# 5. Ticket Issued

Once payment succeeds:

The ticket is generated instantly.

Each ticket includes:

* Unique QR code
* Ticket ID
* Event
* Venue
* Date and time
* Ticket type
* Purchaser name
* Security signature

---

# 6. My Tickets

A dedicated section in the consumer app.

Displays:

* Upcoming
* Past
* Transferred
* Cancelled

Each ticket card shows:

* Event poster
* Venue
* Date
* Ticket status

---

# 7. Ticket Details

Opening a ticket shows:

* QR code
* Event information
* Venue location
* Entry instructions
* Dress code
* Age restriction
* Route button
* Add to calendar

---

# 8. QR Check-in

Venue staff scan tickets using the Creator Studio.

The scanner validates:

* Ticket authenticity
* Event
* Entry status
* Time
* Duplicate scans

The app should support temporary offline validation and sync once connectivity returns.

---

# 9. Ticket Transfer

Users can transfer eligible tickets to another FOMO account.

Flow:

```text
Open Ticket
      │
      ▼
Transfer
      │
      ▼
Select Recipient
      │
      ▼
Recipient Accepts
      │
      ▼
Ownership Updated
```

Rules are defined by the organiser.

---

# 10. Ticket Refunds

Refund policies are configured per event.

Possible options:

* No refunds
* Refund until a specified date
* Full refund
* Partial refund

Refund requests are managed by the organiser through Creator Studio.

---

# 11. Guest Tickets

Creators can issue:

* Complimentary tickets
* Staff passes
* Media passes
* Artist passes
* VIP invitations

Each uses the same secure ticketing infrastructure.

---

# 12. Ticket Status

Every ticket has a lifecycle:

* Reserved
* Paid
* Issued

# 13. Security

Each ticket includes:

* Unique QR code
* Cryptographic signature
* Duplicate detection
* Tamper protection
* Audit log
* Scan history

This reduces fraud and unauthorized entry.

Users receive updates for:

* Purchase confirmation
* Payment receipt

* Schedule changes

* Refund status

# 15. Creator Controls

Organisers can:

* View live ticket sales
* Pause sales
* Release more tickets

* Scan tickets

---

# How FOMO Makes Money

Rather than charging organisers upfront, align revenue with successful sales.

Possible revenue streams include:

1. **Service Fee** – A transparent fee added to each paid ticket.
2. **Platform Fee** – A small percentage retained from each sale.
3. **Promoted Events** – Paid promotion through the Promote module.
4. **Premium Creator Features** – Advanced analytics, team tools, and automation.

This means organisers only pay when they successfully sell tickets.

---

# A Feature That Could Differentiate FOMO

Instead of treating tickets as static QR codes, make them **living tickets**.

A purchased ticket could automatically update with:

* Countdown to doors opening.
* Real-time event announcements.
* Venue notices (e.g., parking changes or last entry).
* Flash Drops available to ticket holders.
* Live navigation to the venue.
* Weather information.
* Entry gate or VIP access instructions.

The ticket becomes a companion throughout the event journey—from purchase, to arrival, to entry—rather than just a barcode. This reinforces FOMO's vision of connecting the entire nightlife experience into one platform while giving users a reason to keep opening the app even after they've bought their ticket.
