I think this is where FOMO can beat competitors.

Don't try to compete with Meta Ads or TikTok Ads on advertising sophistication. Compete on **simplicity, transparency, and affordability**.

Your pricing should make creators think:

> **"It's only R50… why not?"**

Instead of:

> "Should I spend R2,000 on Facebook Ads?"

# The FOMO Pricing Philosophy

## Small, Measurable, Nightlife-Focused

People in nightlife think in terms of **tonight's revenue**, not monthly marketing budgets.

If a club can make an extra **R10,000** tonight, spending **R100–R300** is an easy decision.

# Ticketing Fees

Rather than charging organisers a monthly subscription, consider a small fee per paid ticket sold.

For example:

* Free events: no ticketing fee.
* Paid tickets: a fixed fee or a small percentage per ticket.

This aligns your revenue with the organiser's success.

## A Revenue Model I'd Avoid

I would avoid charging **per click**, **per impression**, or running a real-time bidding auction like Meta or Google.

Nightlife creators don't want to think in CPMs or CPCs. They want to know:

* "How much will this cost?"
* "How long will it run?"
* "What am I promoting?"

Flat, predictable pricing makes purchasing much easier.

---

# A Revenue Model I'd Add

I would introduce **Performance Boost Credits**.

Instead of every purchase requiring a custom campaign, creators buy credit packs:

| Credit Pack   | Price |
| ------------- | ----: |
| 100 Credits   |  R100 |
| 250 Credits   |  R225 |
| 500 Credits   |  R425 |
| 1,000 Credits |  R800 |

Then services simply consume credits:

* Flash Drop Boost: **20 credits**
* Event Boost (Local): **50 credits**
* Event Boost (City): **100 credits**
* Venue Boost (7 days): **100 credits**
* Live Broadcast Boost: **30 credits**

Psychologically, spending **50 credits** feels easier than spending **R50**, and prepaid balances encourage repeat use. It also lets you reward creators with bonus credits for referrals, milestones, or premium subscriptions without issuing cash discounts. This gives FOMO a flexible, creator-friendly monetization system while keeping pricing transparent and approachable.
