# FOMO Creator Studio — Settings Module

## Product Vision

The **Settings Module** is the administrative control center of FOMO Creator Studio.

It allows creators to manage their account, security, business preferences, teams, payments, integrations, notifications, and application preferences from one secure location.

Unlike other modules that focus on operating nightlife experiences, the Settings Module focuses on **how creators manage their Creator Studio account and organization**.

It should be designed for clarity, security, and long-term account management.

---

# Design Principles

The Settings Module should be:

* Secure by default.
* Role-aware, adapting to creator permissions.
* Simple to navigate despite advanced functionality.
* Centralized, avoiding scattered settings throughout the app.
* Enterprise-ready, supporting businesses with multiple staff members.
* Future-proof for new integrations and platform features.

---

# Guiding Principle

The Settings Module should feel like the **administrative headquarters** of Creator Studio. It should centralize everything that governs a creator's account, business, team, security, and platform preferences without cluttering operational workflows. Every setting should reinforce trust, reduce administrative overhead, and give creators confidence that their business is secure, well-managed, and ready to grow within the FOMO ecosystem.
