# FOMO Creator Studio — Intelligence Module

## Product Vision

The **Intelligence Module** is the business intelligence and decision-making engine of FOMO Creator Studio.

Unlike traditional analytics dashboards that only display historical data, Intelligence transforms data from every Creator Studio module into **actionable insights, predictions, and recommendations** that help nightlife professionals make smarter decisions.

It answers questions such as:

* *Which event format performs best?*
* *When should I launch my Flash Drop?*
* *Why did ticket sales slow down?*
* *Which DJs attract the biggest crowds?*
* *Which promotions generate the highest ROI?*
* *What should I do next?*

The Intelligence Module becomes the creator's **AI-powered business advisor**, helping venues, organisers, DJs, and artists continuously improve their performance.

---

# Design Principles

The Intelligence Module should be:

* Insight-driven, not chart-driven.
* Actionable, with every insight leading to a recommendation.
* Predictive, using historical and live data to forecast outcomes.
* Easy to understand, avoiding overly technical language.
* Real-time where appropriate.
* Privacy-conscious, exposing only data creators are authorized to access.

# Security & Privacy

The Intelligence Module should:

* Respect creator permissions.
* Never expose another creator's confidential business data.
* Aggregate benchmarks anonymously.
* Encrypt sensitive analytics data.
* Log access to business reports where appropriate.

AI recommendations should always explain **why** a recommendation is being made.

# Guiding Principle

The Intelligence Module should evolve from a reporting dashboard into the **AI operating brain of FOMO Creator Studio**. Rather than overwhelming creators with charts and numbers, it should surface the most important insights, explain what they mean, predict what is likely to happen next, and recommend the best actions to take. Every recommendation should be grounded in the creator's own data and integrated across Events, Venue, Ticketing, Flash Drops, Marketing, and Audience, making Intelligence the strategic decision-making layer that powers the entire nightlife ecosystem.
