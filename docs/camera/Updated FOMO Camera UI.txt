# FOMO Camera Engine

> **Vision**
>
> The FOMO Camera is **not** a social media camera.
>
> It is the **world's first nightlife-aware camera**, designed to disappear into the background while intelligently understanding where the user is, what's happening around them, who they're with, the music, the crowd, and the atmosphere.
>
> It should feel as simple as Apple's Camera while quietly performing dozens of AI-powered tasks in the background. 

---

# Design Principles

The camera follows one philosophy:

> **Capture first.
> Everything else stays out of the way.**

Core principles:

* Instant launch (<250 ms)
* Full-screen immersive preview
* One-handed operation
* Minimal interface
* Context-aware
* Venue-aware
* Event-aware
* AI-assisted
* Sound-reactive
* Creator-ready
* Offline-first
* Privacy-first

# Location Visibility

This replaces the old "Followers" label.

It is **not** a follower count.

It controls **who can see the venue attached to the Moment after publishing**.

---

# Final Vision

The FOMO Camera should feel invisible. Users open it and immediately start capturing without thinking about settings, tagging, or workflow. The interface surfaces only the essentials: venue context, location-sharing preference, Flash, Studio, zoom, and capture controls. Everything else—from venue detection and event association to AI enhancement, music awareness, and publishing—is handled intelligently in the background, delivering a camera purpose-built for nightlife that remains as approachable as a default smartphone camera.
