FOMO Local-First Camera OS

Production Implementation Plan (v2.0)

Vision

The FOMO Camera is a Local-First Camera Operating System built specifically for nightlife and real-world experiences. The entire camera works without internet while seamlessly synchronizing with Supabase and Telegram whenever connectivity becomes available.

The user should never know—or care—whether they are online or offline.

This extends the existing FOMO Camera Engine by making the device the primary runtime, while Supabase and Telegram become synchronization services rather than runtime dependencies. It also aligns with the existing Offline Engine and Upload Engine in your Camera architecture.

Final Vision

The FOMO Local-First Camera OS transforms the camera into a resilient, production-grade platform where the device is always the primary execution environment. Photos, videos, Live sessions, AI processing, editing, venue detection, drafts, and media management all function without connectivity. When a network becomes available, the Background Sync Engine uploads media to Telegram, synchronizes metadata with Supabase, performs online event verification, updates Ripple, and refreshes the Feed, Discover, Map, Club Lobby, and Profile automatically.

By treating venue intelligence as offline, event intelligence as cloud-only, and Supabase and Telegram as synchronization services, the camera remains fast, reliable, and ready for real-world nightlife conditions where connectivity can be inconsistent.
