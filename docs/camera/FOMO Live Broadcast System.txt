FOMO Live Engine

Billion-Dollar Scale Place-Centric Live Experience Platform

Vision

One Sun. Billion Eyes.

FOMO Live is not a traditional livestreaming platform.

It is not creator-centric.

It is place-centric.

Every Live Broadcast represents a real-world moment happening at a venue, event, city, or destination.

Users don't join a livestream.

They simply experience what's happening there right now.

FOMO Live is a Live Camera Render Engine that captures one live camera and intelligently distributes it across the FOMO ecosystem.


---

Core Principles

One broadcaster per Live.

Viewers never join the broadcast.

No guest streaming.

No co-hosting.

No viewer video.

Every broadcaster owns their own Live.

Live Link connects independent broadcasters.

Every Live records locally first.

AI assists but never controls the viewer.

Every Live belongs to a place.

One Camera.

One Place.

One Sun. Billion Eyes.

FOMO Broadcast Engine

The FOMO Broadcast Engine receives one Live Camera Render from the broadcaster.

Responsibilities

Authenticate broadcaster

Validate venue

Validate event

Start broadcast

Publish Live metadata

Update viewer count

Handle comments

Handle reactions

Handle polls

Forward video to the Edge Distribution Engine


The broadcaster uploads only one stream regardless of audience size.


---

The Edge Distribution Engine distributes the Live Camera Render to viewers worldwide.

Responsibilities

Final Vision

FOMO Live is a real-time place rendering platform, not a traditional livestreaming service.

Every broadcast is anchored to a real venue or event. The broadcaster captures a single Live Camera Render, which is distributed through the FOMO Broadcast Engine and Edge Distribution Engine to the Feed, Venue, Event, Discover, Map, and Profile screens. Viewers never join the broadcast. They simply watch, react, comment, and experience what's happening in real time.

One Camera. One Place. One Sun. Billion Eyes.
