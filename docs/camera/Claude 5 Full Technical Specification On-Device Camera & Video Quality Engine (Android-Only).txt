# Full Technical Specification: On-Device Camera & Video Quality Engine (Android-Only)

**Version:** 7.0 — Flagship Production Grade, Video-Elevated
**Target:** Android 12+ (optimized for Android 16+)
**Goal:** A computational photo *and* video engine for nightlife content that beats Google Pixel, Samsung Galaxy, Apple iPhone 17, TikTok, and Instagram capture pipelines — on both stills and short-form video, since that's what nightlife/social content actually is.

**Hard Constraints (unchanged):**
- 100% on-device. Zero cloud inference. Zero image/video telemetry.
- Total on-device model footprint ≤ 105 MB. **(Held firm — video reuses photo models; see Section 19.)**
- Single-image latency: FAST < 800 ms, BALANCED < 4 s, MAXIMUM < 10 s on Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 equivalent.
- Video: real-time preview at capture resolution; post-capture enhancement pass < 1.5x clip duration on flagship tier.

## 1. High-Level Objectives

- Build a complete imaging stack from sensor RAW through JPEG/Ultra HDR *and* through stabilized, color-graded video (H.265/AV1).
- Zero-copy GPU-native pipeline using `AHardwareBuffer`, Vulkan compute, and GPU textures — shared by photo and video paths.
- Plugin-based AI execution graph with dependency resolution, memory pooling, and stage-level sandboxing, with stateless models shared across photo and video.
- Multi-frame RAW fusion with hybrid HDR *and* per-pixel frequency-domain ("Photon Fusion") reconstruction.
- Dedicated AI ISP layer for RAW demosaic, denoise, and color correction, running per-frame in both photo and video modes.
- Monocular depth estimation for realistic portrait bokeh, reused for video depth-of-field effects.
- Zero Shutter Lag capture: continuous pre-shutter frame ring buffer so the "decisive moment" can be selected from *before* the shutter press.
- Creator Look Engine: 3D LUT-based stylistic grading (near-zero storage cost) so output has an identity, not just corrected accuracy.
- Per-lens tuning profiles with full calibration, distortion, vignette, and color profiles.
- Formal camera *and* video state machines with clear lifecycle boundaries.
- Android Ultra HDR (JPEG_R) for photo; HDR10/HLG-tagged video with SDR fallback; full Display P3 color management across both.
- AI confidence thresholds, per-stage quality evaluation, and automatic rollback for both modalities.
- Learn per-device behavior via a split learning database without ever uploading images or video.

## 3. Camera State Machine (Photo)

```
[IDLE] ──open()──> [PREVIEW] ──(ZSL ring buffer active)──┐
                      │                                    │
                      ▼                                    │
              [PRECAPTURE] ──meter/focus──> [PRECAPTURE_READY]
                      │                           │
                      ▼                           ▼
              [CAPTURE] <───────────────── [PRECAPTURE_READY]
                      │        (may pull frames from ZSL ring, pre-dating shutter press)
                      ▼
              [PROCESSING] ──cancel──> [READY]
                      │
                      ▼
              [POSTPROCESS]
                      │
                      ▼
                  [SAVING]
                      │
                      ▼
                  [READY]
```

**Zero Shutter Lag (ZSL) addition:** During `PREVIEW`, a ring buffer (`ZslRingPool`, 8 frames, ~1.2 s at 30fps) continuously retains recent YUV/RAW frames with metadata. On shutter press, `CAPTURE` can select frames from *before* the physical press (subject peak expression, dance move apex) in addition to frames captured after. Selection is scored by the existing Quality Evaluator + a new lightweight motion-peak heuristic (Stage 0B) — no new model required.

### 3B. Video State Machine (NEW)

```
[IDLE] ──open()──> [VIDEO_PREVIEW]
                      │
                      ▼
              [RECORDING] ──(stream: capture → stabilize → temporal denoise
                      │        → temporal HDR/tone map → Look LUT → encode)
                      ▼
              [RECORDING_PAUSED] <──resume──> [RECORDING]
                      │
                      ▼
              [FINALIZING] ──(flush encoder, mux, write EXIF/metadata)──> [READY]
```

Rules:
- `RECORDING` runs a *streaming* version of the photo AI graph: per-frame scene/segmentation/lighting run at reduced cadence (every 2nd–4th frame, propagated via optical flow in between) to hit real-time budgets.
- Stabilization and temporal denoise run every frame; they are the cheapest, highest-impact stages for user-perceived video quality.
- `FINALIZING` may run one more full-quality enhancement pass (BALANCED/MAXIMUM tier) on the encoded buffer if the device tier and thermal state allow, similar to "processed after the fact" behavior in Pixel's video HDR.
- Only one `RECORDING` or photo `PROCESSING` session may exist at a time; switching modes mid-session requires returning to `READY` first.

## 4. Zero-Copy Image & Video Pipeline

Eliminate all `Bitmap` ↔ `Tensor` copies for both stills and video frames.

```
Camera2 ImageReader (RAW10/YUV)  ──or──  Video frame stream (YUV_420_888 @ target fps)
        │
        ▼
AHardwareBuffer (GPU importable)
        │
        ▼
Vulkan Compute Image / GPU Texture
        │
        ├──► OpenCV GPU (alignment, optical flow, tracking)
        │
        ├──► LiteRT GPU Delegate (AI inference — shared models, photo or video mode)
        │
        ├──► Vulkan Compute (HDR merge, Photon Fusion, tone map, stabilization warp, LUT apply)
        │
        ▼
HardwareBuffer Encoder (JPEG / JPEG_R)  ──or──  MediaCodec Surface Input (H.265/AV1, HDR10/HLG)
        │
        ▼
Output File / Surface
```

**Buffer Contracts (extended):**
- All intermediate stages read/write `AHardwareBuffer` with `VK_FORMAT_R16G16B16A16_SFLOAT` or `VK_FORMAT_R8G8B8A8_UNORM`.
- Video adds a `TemporalRingPool`: 4 buffers holding the last N processed frames + motion vectors, feeding recurrent denoise/HDR without CPU round-trips.
- CPU access only for: EXIF/metadata writing, final file I/O, and LiteRT CPU fallback.
- Memory Pools:
  - `FramePool`: 16 buffers for burst frames.
  - `TensorPool`: 8 buffers for model I/O.
  - `TilePool`: 32 buffers for SR tiling.
  - `GpuBufferPool`: 12 buffers for Vulkan compute intermediates.
  - `ZslRingPool`: 8 buffers, continuously recycled during `PREVIEW`.
  - `TemporalRingPool`: 4 buffers, active only during `RECORDING`.

Fallback: If Vulkan is unavailable, fall back to `OpenGL ES 3.1` compute shaders + `ImageReader`/`Allocation`. If GLES unavailable, fall back to `RenderScript` → finally `Bitmap` CPU path. Video fallback path disables temporal denoise (too CPU-expensive) and keeps only stabilization + basic tone mapping.

## 7. Processing Pipeline — Photo (All Stages)

*(Stages -3 through 12 carry over from v6.0 with the following insertions and modifications. Unlisted stages are unchanged.)*

**Stage 0: Motion Analysis & Hybrid HDR Fusion — enhanced with Photon Fusion**
- Motion analysis and adaptive HDR strategy table unchanged.
- **New: Photon Fusion (per-pixel frequency-domain merge).** After frame-level hybrid HDR merge, decompose the merged image and the sharpest single frame into low/high frequency bands (Laplacian pyramid, Vulkan compute — no ML weights). Take low-frequency (tone, color, exposure) from the multi-frame merge; take high-frequency (fine detail, texture) from the single sharpest frame, guided by the segmentation mask so skin gets gentler detail injection than hair/fabric/jewelry. This is algorithmically equivalent to Apple's Deep Fusion approach and costs zero additional model MB.

**Stage 0B: Peak Moment Selector (NEW)**
- Runs across ZSL ring buffer frames + capture burst.
- Reuses the existing Quality Evaluator model (Stage 11) at low resolution per candidate frame, plus a lightweight algorithmic "expression/motion apex" heuristic using existing BlazeFace landmarks (eye openness, mouth motion delta, body motion score minimum = likely peak of a dance move or expression).
- Selects the base frame for HDR/Photon Fusion, rather than always defaulting to shutter-press timing.

**Stage 0C: Low-Light Long-Exposure Mode (NEW)**
- Triggered when Scene Classifier detects `Outdoor` + `Street Night` or `UNKNOWN` low-light with camera motion score < 1 px (tripod/braced) and ambient lux estimate below threshold.
- Extends burst to up to 15 frames at longer per-frame exposure, aligns via optical flow, accumulates in linear RAW space (same Photon Fusion machinery, tuned for noise floor reduction over detail injection). No new model required.

**Stage 3B: Monocular Depth Estimation (NEW)**
- Model: lightweight MobileNet-based monocular depth network (e.g., FastDepth-style architecture), INT8, ~2.5 MB.
- Input: RGB + existing segmentation mask (person/background separation as a prior, improving depth quality on relative-depth-only mobile models).
- Output: normalized relative depth map.
- Consumer: physically-modeled defocus blur (circle-of-confusion by depth, not just a segmentation cutout), giving portrait/background separation an actual lens-like falloff instead of a flat bokeh disc — the single biggest visible gap vs. iPhone/Pixel portrait mode.
- Also feeds Stage 7 (enhancer) as an auxiliary input for depth-aware tone mapping (foreground pop).

**Stage 7: End-to-End RGB Enhancement** — unchanged model, now also receives the depth map as an input channel.

**Stage 12B: Creator Look Engine (NEW)**
- Runs after Stage 12's standard tone mapping, before final encode.
- Implementation: 3D LUTs (33×33×33, ~140 KB each as data, not model weights) applied via Vulkan compute shader trilinear interpolation.
- Ships with a small curated set (e.g., "Neon Night," "Warm Club," "High-Contrast B&W," "Natural") tuned specifically against nightlife scene classes, distinct per Scene Classifier output.
- User-selectable at capture or apply-after; intensity slider blends toward the LUT-graded result via linear interpolation with the corrected pre-LUT image, so it never fights the accuracy stages above it.
- Cost: near-zero model budget (LUTs are lookup tables, not learned weights) — this is how you get an "Instagram/TikTok has a look" identity without spending your MB budget.

All existing Stage 11 rollback logic (`enhanced.overall < original.overall * 0.92` → rollback) applies unchanged; Photon Fusion and Look Engine outputs are separately evaluated so a bad LUT choice never triggers a false quality rollback of the underlying accurate image (the pre-LUT image is what's evaluated).

## 7B. Processing Pipeline — Video (NEW, First-Class)

Video reuses the photo pipeline's stateless models (Scene Classifier, Segmentation, Depth, Enhancer) rather than duplicating weights. New work is concentrated in temporal coherence, which is fundamentally an algorithmic/compute problem, not a new-model problem — this is what keeps the whole thing inside the 105 MB ceiling.

**V-Stage 0: Capture & Hybrid EIS Stabilization**
- Fuse hardware OIS position + gyroscope (200 Hz) + software optical-flow-derived motion vectors.
- Rolling-shutter correction: per-scanline warp compensation using gyro data timestamped to scanline readout.
- Crop margin: adaptive 5–12% based on motion energy, to leave warp headroom without a visible "shaky crop jump."

**V-Stage 1: Streaming AI ISP**
- Same 4 MB AI ISP model as photo, run every frame (cheap enough at video resolution/INT8 to sustain real-time).

**V-Stage 2: Reduced-Cadence Semantic Understanding**
- Scene Classifier + Segmentation + Depth run every 2nd–4th frame (device-tier dependent); results propagated to intermediate frames via optical flow warp of the mask/depth map, not re-inference. This is standard practice for real-time video segmentation and avoids 4x-ing NPU cost.

**V-Stage 3: Temporal Denoiser**
- Recurrent denoising: current frame + optical-flow-warped previous *denoised* output, blended with a per-pixel confidence weight (lower weight where flow confidence is low, e.g., occlusion or fast motion, to avoid ghosting).
- Reuses the Enhancer model's denoise sub-path in a recurrent wrapper — no new weights, only a new algorithmic recurrence loop and the `TemporalRingPool`.
- This is the single highest-leverage stage for beating TikTok/Instagram video quality: their re-encode compression destroys shadow detail; clean input before their re-encode holds up far better.

**V-Stage 4: Temporal HDR / Local Tone Mapping**
- Per-frame local tone mapping (not just global exposure) with a temporal smoothing term to prevent flicker between frames — critical for strobing club lighting, which naturally has huge frame-to-frame exposure variance.
- Where device supports it, tag output as HLG or HDR10 video; otherwise tone-map to SDR Display P3.

**V-Stage 5: Motion Tracker**
- Lightweight algorithmic tracker (Kalman filter / IoU-based association) over existing Face Detection + Object Detection outputs across frames — reuses detection models, adds no new weight cost.
- Powers stable face-locked exposure/WB across a clip (prevents the "AE hunts every time someone dances into frame" problem) and stable depth-aware bokeh continuity.

**V-Stage 6: Creator Look Engine (Video)**
- Same LUT engine as Stage 12B, applied per-frame with the same temporal-smoothing discipline as V-Stage 4 to avoid look "pumping."

**V-Stage 7: Encode**
- MediaCodec surface input, H.265 (broad compatibility) or AV1 (where hardware-accelerated, better quality/bitrate), HDR10/HLG metadata tagging when applicable.
- Optional: post-record "finishing pass" (FINALIZING state) re-runs Enhancer + Look Engine at higher quality on the already-stabilized/denoised buffer if thermal/tier budget allows — analogous to how some flagships process video HDR after the fact rather than only live.

**Video Quality Evaluator & Rollback**
- Same rollback discipline as photo: per-segment (e.g., every 2s) quality scoring; if a segment's processed quality falls below a threshold relative to the stabilized-but-unenhanced baseline, that segment falls back to the lighter processing tier rather than corrupting the whole clip.

## 8. AI Execution Graph & Plugin System

Plugin interface and `ExecutionGraphBuilder` structure unchanged from v6.0, extended with a parallel `VideoExecutionGraphBuilder` that wires the V-Stages above and explicitly marks which nodes run every frame vs. every Nth frame:

```kotlin
val videoGraph = VideoExecutionGraphBuilder()
    .addNode(StabilizationStage(), cadence = EVERY_FRAME)
    .addNode(AiIspStage(), cadence = EVERY_FRAME)
    .addNode(SceneClassifierStage(), cadence = everyNFrames(4))
    .addNode(SegmentationStage().dependsOn("scene"), cadence = everyNFrames(2))
    .addNode(DepthStage().dependsOn("segment"), cadence = everyNFrames(2))
    .addNode(TemporalDenoiseStage().dependsOn("isp"), cadence = EVERY_FRAME)
    .addNode(TemporalHdrStage().dependsOn("denoise"), cadence = EVERY_FRAME)
    .addNode(MotionTrackerStage().dependsOn("detect"), cadence = EVERY_FRAME)
    .addNode(LookEngineStage().dependsOn("hdr"), cadence = EVERY_FRAME)
    .build()
```

## 9. Scheduler Architecture

Unchanged three-scheduler model (`PipelineScheduler`, `WorkScheduler`, `TileScheduler`), plus:

| Scheduler | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `VideoFrameScheduler` (NEW) | Enforces per-frame real-time deadline; if a frame's AI stages would miss the deadline, drops to cadence-skip (reuse last propagated result) rather than dropping the frame from output. |

Concurrency rule addition: `CRITICAL` video stages (stabilization warp, AI ISP, temporal denoise) get dedicated threads and must never be skipped even under thermal pressure — they're cheap and are the main perceptual quality driver; semantic stages (scene/segment/depth/detect) are the first to degrade cadence under load.

## 22. Success Criteria

All v6.0 photo criteria apply unchanged. Video success criteria (NEW):

- A handheld nightclub video clip must show: stable framing (no visible micro-jitter), clean shadow detail that survives social-platform re-encoding, no strobing-light flicker, consistent face exposure as the subject moves through frame, and a discernible "look" without crushed highlights on neon signage.
- Portrait/selfie photos must show depth-correct bokeh falloff (not a flat cutout), visible via a gradient blur test chart at graduated distances.
- ZSL-selected frames must, in blind A/B testing, be preferred over strict shutter-timed frames in >60% of motion/expression scenarios.
- Safety: > 95% rollback success rate when AI degrades quality, for both photo and video.
- Performance: Photo BALANCED < 5 s on Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 without throttling; Video live pipeline sustains 30 FPS on Flagship tier without frame drops under NORMAL thermal state.
- Portability: Runs on Samsung, Google Pixel, and Xiaomi/OnePlus families with automatic feature negotiation, for both photo and video paths.
