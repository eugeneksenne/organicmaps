# Product Vision

The Wellness Lobby is the premium digital storefront for health, wellness, recovery, and fitness businesses on FOMO.

Unlike the Club Lobby, which is about nightlife, or the Prep Lobby, which is about appearance, the Wellness Lobby is about **health, relaxation, recovery, and self-care**.

Within **10 seconds**, users should know:

* Is this business right for me?
* What wellness services do they offer?
* How much do they cost?
* Can I trust them?
* How do I request an appointment?

**Primary Goal**

Convert discovery into an appointment request.

# Design Philosophy

The Wellness Lobby should feel **calm, premium, and restorative**. Soft imagery, clean layouts, and trust-building information should take priority over promotional content. Unlike the Prep Lobby, where **Looks** drive conversion, the Wellness Lobby is centred on **Treatments**, **Experts**, and **wellbeing outcomes**. The experience should reassure users that they're choosing qualified professionals while making it effortless to request an appointment. Official businesses remain lightweight with a **Visit Website** experience, while claimed and FOMO-created businesses unlock the complete Wellness Lobby and Wellness Dashboard.
