Product Vision

The Prep Lobby is the premium digital storefront for beauty, grooming, and self-care businesses on FOMO.

Unlike the Club Lobby, which creates excitement for nightlife, the Prep Lobby creates confidence by showcasing craftsmanship, quality, and style.

Within 10 seconds, users should know:

Is this business worth visiting?

What styles do they specialize in?

How much do services cost?

Can I trust their work?

How do I request an appointment?

Primary Goal

Design Principles

Looks are the hero, not the business.

Every look should inspire confidence and drive an appointment request.

Businesses showcase craftsmanship rather than marketing.

Navigation is fast through sticky tabs.

Social proof comes from authentic signals such as verified visits, trending looks, and featured creators.

V1 focuses on requesting appointments, avoiding the complexity of full booking while leaving a seamless upgrade path to a future scheduling engine.

Official businesses remain lightweight with a Visit Website experience, while claimed and FOMO-created businesses unlock the full Prep Lobby and Prep Dashboard.
