Product Vision

The Add Business engine is the gateway for businesses to join the FOMO ecosystem.

It serves two purposes:

Allow owners to claim existing businesses already on the map.

Allow owners to create brand-new businesses that don't yet exist.

The onboarding experience should take less than 3 minutes.

No paperwork.

No manual verification.

Ownership is verified using OTP.

Core Principles

Search before creating to prevent duplicates.

Claiming existing businesses is always free.

Existing businesses are verified using masked contact details and OTP.

If no contact details exist, owners add and verify them without creating a duplicate listing.

Only brand-new businesses pay the one-time R50 verification fee.

Every verified business receives a category-specific Lobby and Dashboard.

No subscriptions, no paperwork, and no manual verification—just a fast, secure, OTP-based onboarding experience.
