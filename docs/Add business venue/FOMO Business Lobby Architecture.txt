FOMO Business Lobby Architecture

The Digital Front Door for Every Business

Vision

Every business on FOMO has a Lobby.

A Lobby is not a static business listing.

It is the business's living digital presence inside FOMO.

Users should be able to understand a business in under 10 seconds.

Each industry has a lobby specifically designed around how customers interact with that business.

Core Product Principle

FOMO is not a directory of businesses.

It is a platform where every verified business has a living, interactive lobby designed for its industry.

Whether someone is looking for a nightclub, a barber, a street food vendor, a spa, or a hotel, they should stay inside FOMO to discover, compare, trust, and engage with the business—without being redirected elsewhere.

This architecture creates one consistent experience across the app while allowing each industry to have specialized features that match how customers actually choose and interact with those businesses.
