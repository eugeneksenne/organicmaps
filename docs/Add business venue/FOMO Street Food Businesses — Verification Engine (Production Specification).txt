Trust & Safety

Verification can be suspended or revoked if a business:

Uses false information

Impersonates another business

Commits payment fraud

Repeatedly violates food safety or platform policies

Receives verified reports of serious misconduct after investigation

This protects customers while preserving the value of the verified badge.
