# FOMO Ripple Engine

## Full Production Implementation Plan (Billion-Dollar Scale)

> **A Like says "I like this."**
>
> **A Ripple says "More people should experience this."**

The Ripple Engine is FOMO's signature social intelligence system. It measures **real-world momentum**, not popularity. Every Ripple contributes to a living ecosystem that powers recommendations, venue discovery, event discovery, creator growth, and city activity.

Ripple is **not a reaction**.

Ripple is **FOMO's momentum engine.**

---

# Core Philosophy

Every Ripple answers one question:

> **"Is this experience worth someone else's time?"**

Unlike likes, Ripples influence discovery across the entire platform.

A Ripple should always feel intentional.

# Ripple Velocity

Momentum depends on **speed**, not only totals.

Example:

100 Ripples over 24 hours

is weaker than

100 Ripples in 20 minutes.

Velocity heavily influences ranking.

---

# Ripple Decay

Momentum naturally decreases over time.

Benefits:

* Fresh discovery
* Fair exposure
* Less stale content
* Dynamic recommendations

Older Moments can still remain visible through other signals, but Ripple influence fades unless renewed by continued engagement.

# Success Criteria

The Ripple Engine is considered production-ready only when it:

* Preserves the unique meaning of a Ripple as a recommendation, not a Like.
* Uses one consistent momentum model across Moments, venues, events, and cities.
* Drives discovery across every major FOMO surface through a single source of truth.
* Maintains strong privacy boundaries by remaining independent of **Moment Invitations** and **Who's Here**.
* Is resilient against spam, bots, and coordinated manipulation.
* Delivers low-latency updates while scaling to millions of users and interactions.
* Remains simple for users—a single tap—but sophisticated behind the scenes through a robust, realtime momentum engine.

This design makes Ripple a foundational platform capability rather than just another engagement metric, giving FOMO a distinctive social signal that connects digital moments with real-world experiences.
