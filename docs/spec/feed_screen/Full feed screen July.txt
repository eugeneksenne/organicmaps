# FOMO Feed System

## Full Updated Production Implementation Plan (Billion-Dollar Scale)

> **People create Moments.**
>
> **Moments create FOMO.**
>
> **FOMO turns digital discovery into real-world experiences.**

The FOMO Feed is a premium, full-screen, media-first experience where every Moment has the potential to inspire someone to visit a venue, attend an event, discover a restaurant, or connect with people in real life. The feed is powered by the Ripple Engine and enhanced by contextual Moment Invitations that connect content with physical places.

---

# Core Product Principles

The Feed is built around six principles:

* Moments are always the hero.
* Premium simplicity over visual clutter.
* Realtime momentum over historical popularity.
* Discovery should always lead to action.
* Intelligence remains invisible.
* Privacy is always user-controlled.

# Moment Invitation Card

The Moment Invitation Card is created from the **Camera** when the creator chooses to attach a location and share an invitation.

It is **not** a venue information card.

It represents a temporary invitation to join the creator's current experience.

The card always remains attached to the Moment while the venue exists.

Only its state changes.

---

## Creating an Invitation

Camera Flow:

```text
Camera

↓

Record Moment

↓

Attach Location

↓

Share Moment Invitation

↓

Choose Duration

15 min

30 min

1 hour

2 hours

Until I Leave

↓

Publish
```

The chosen duration determines how long the invitation remains active.

---

# Moment Invitation States

## State 1 — Active Invitation

The creator is still available.

```text
📍 Cocoon Nightclub ✓

🟢 Alfred is here now

Available for

04:45

[ Club Lobby ]   [ Route ]
```

The countdown represents **remaining invitation availability**, not venue closing time.

---

## State 2 — Invitation Ended

The creator leaves, manually ends sharing, or the invitation expires.

```text
📍 Cocoon Nightclub ✓

⚪ Alfred has left

[ Club Lobby ]   [ Route ]
```

The invitation has ended.

The venue remains available.

Users can still:

* Open Club Lobby
* Navigate to the venue
* Discover ongoing activity

---

## State 3 — Venue Closed

Venue intelligence detects the venue is currently closed.

```text
📍 Cocoon Nightclub ✓

🔴 Venue Closed

Opens Friday 18:00

[ Club Lobby ]   [ Route ]
```

Users may still view the venue and plan a future visit.

# Camera & Invitation Relationship

The Moment Invitation is configured entirely within the Camera.

Background location does **not** automatically create invitations.

The creator explicitly chooses:

* Whether to share an invitation.
* How long it remains active.

---

# Venue Presence System ("Who's Here")

This system is **independent** from the Feed.

It does **not** affect the Moment Invitation.

After approximately 5 minutes inside a venue geofence, FOMO asks:

```text
You're at

📍 Cocoon Nightclub

Would you like to appear in

Who's Here?

○ Public

○ Followers

○ Friends

● Private (Default)

[ Share Presence ]

[ Not Now ]
```

This only controls visibility inside the venue's **Who's Here** section.

It does **not** modify feed posts or Moment Invitations.

## Final Architecture

The Feed now has three clearly separated systems:

1. **Moments** — the content users create and consume.
2. **Moment Invitation Card** — a temporary invitation created from the Camera that invites others to join the creator while they're still there, then gracefully transitions to show that the creator has left while preserving venue actions.
3. **Venue Presence ("Who's Here")** — an independent, privacy-first system that lets users opt into being visible at a venue after they've been there for a few minutes, with **Private** as the default.

This separation keeps the feed focused on storytelling, the invitation focused on real-time social connection, and venue presence focused on user-controlled visibility, creating a scalable architecture that fits FOMO's vision of connecting digital moments with real-world experiences.
