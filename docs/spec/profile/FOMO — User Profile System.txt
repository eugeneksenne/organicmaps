# FOMO Profile System

## The Digital Passport

---

# Product Vision

The FOMO Profile is not a traditional social media profile.

It is a **living digital passport** that evolves with a person's real-world experiences.

Unlike Instagram, which captures who you were, FOMO reflects:

* Who you are
* Where you've been
* What you enjoy
* The experiences that define your lifestyle

The profile should feel:

* Alive
* Premium
* Cinematic
* Personal
* Trustworthy
* Dynamic

A visitor should understand someone's social identity in **under five seconds**.

---

# Design Principles

* **Identity over popularity**
* **Experiences over content**
* **Presence over activity**
* **Privacy by design**
* **AI enhances, never replaces**
* **Premium simplicity**

---

# Live Presence

The signature feature of FOMO.

A floating premium card beneath the hero.

---

## Presence Status

Simple, clear, and privacy-friendly.

### ?? Online

The user is active on FOMO.

---

### ?? Busy

The user prefers not to be disturbed.

Can be set manually or suggested automatically when attending events or using Focus Mode.

---

### ? Offline

The user is inactive or has chosen to appear offline.

---

## Live Presence Card

Example

```
?? Online

?? Konka

?? Amapiano

?? With 5 Friends

Outside for 2h

[Route]
```

Privacy enabled

```
?? Busy

?? Location Hidden

Outside Tonight
```

Offline

```
? Offline

Last Active

Yesterday
```

Presence Status answers:

**"Are they available?"**

Live Presence answers:

**"What are they experiencing?"**

# Final Positioning

The FOMO Profile is **the digital passport of a person's nightlife life**. It is not a collection of posts or follower counts, but a living identity built from authentic experiences, meaningful places, memorable moments, and real-world connections. By combining AI-powered curation, privacy-first presence, immersive storytelling, and premium design, it becomes the central hub of the entire FOMO ecosystem—where every venue, event, friendship, and memory contributes to an evolving story that feels deeply personal and unmistakably alive.
