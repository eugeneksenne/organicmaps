FOMO Universal Venue Preview System (Updated)

Product Vision

The Universal Venue Preview System is FOMO's standard interaction model for every venue in the app.

Regardless of where a venue is discovered, users always experience the same premium preview, making the app predictable, fast, and consistent. The layout never changes—only the venue data, imagery, category chips, and contextual actions adapt to the venue type. This builds on the design goal of a lightweight preview that bridges browsing and the full venue profile.

Design Principles

One reusable component across the entire app.

Fast browsing with minimal cognitive load.

Rich information before entering a venue profile.

Context-aware actions.

Consistent animations and interactions.

Premium Apple-quality UI.

Universal Interaction Flow

Motion & Animation

When a venue is tapped:

Expand from the source card.

Dim and blur the background.

Elevate the preview with a soft shadow.

Smoothly scale the hero image.

Fade in the dynamic chips.

Pulse the live status indicator.

Dismiss by tapping outside or swiping down.

The motion remains consistent regardless of venue category.

Performance

Progressive hero image loading.

Cached venue metadata.

Lazy-loaded high-resolution images.

Hardware-accelerated animations.

Smooth scrolling in horizontal lists.

Responsive performance on lower-end devices.

Final Design Philosophy

The Universal Venue Preview Card becomes one of FOMO's core design system components. Every venue—whether discovered through Explore the City, Smart Places, Closing Soon, Flash Drops, Trending Now, Search, or the Map—uses the same premium interaction pattern. Users learn it once, and the app simply swaps the venue data, imagery, chips, and contextual actions based on the venue type. This creates a scalable, maintainable, and consistently premium discovery experience across the entire platform.
