FOMO — Prep Rooms
Billion-Dollar Product Specification
Status: Production Design
Purpose: Help users prepare for nightlife events together through inspiration, coordination, education, and social interaction.

1. Vision
Prep Rooms is the pre-nightlife social experience of FOMO.
It is where users prepare for a night out by discovering inspiration, learning new looks, coordinating with friends, and sharing their preparation journey.
It bridges the gap between:
Choosing an event → Arriving at the venue.
Prep Rooms is not a beauty marketplace or social network.
It is a preparation ecosystem built specifically for nightlife.

2. Core Principles
Everything inside Prep Rooms must help users prepare for going out.
Every feature should answer at least one question:
What should I wear?
What look fits this venue?
How are my friends preparing?
How should I do my makeup?
What hairstyle should I try?
Where can I prepare nearby?
What do people normally wear here?
If it doesn't improve preparation, it doesn't belong.

Final Product Positioning
Prep Rooms is no longer just a feed for outfit polls. It becomes FOMO's preparation engine—the place where users coordinate with friends, discover venue-specific inspiration, learn through tutorials, save ideas, and complete everything they need before stepping out. It naturally connects Discover, Events, My Circle, Smart Places, Camera, and Creator Studio into one seamless pre-nightlife experience while keeping the product focused on its core mission: helping people prepare for unforgettable nights out.
