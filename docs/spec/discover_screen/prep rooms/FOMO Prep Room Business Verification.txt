FOMO Prep Room Business Verification

Step 1 — Create Business (Free)

Anyone can add a Prep Room business to FOMO for free.

They provide:

Business name

Category (Barber, Salon, Nail Studio, Makeup Artist, Spa, etc.)

Address

Contact details

Business hours

Photos

Services

Pricing (optional)

Social media links

The business appears on FOMO as:

Unverified Business

Step 2 — Verify Business (R50 Once-Off)

Businesses can unlock verification for a once-off payment of R50.

After payment and successful verification, they receive:

✅ Verified badge

✅ Higher placement in search results

✅ Eligible for AI recommendations

✅ Can receive bookings

✅ Business dashboard

✅ Analytics

✅ Flash Deals

✅ Promotions

✅ Customer reviews

✅ Ability to respond to reviews

✅ Eligible for "Top Rated" and "Trending" sections

Why users should verify

Rather than presenting it as paying for a badge, frame it as unlocking professional tools.

For example:

Verify Your Business

Build trust with customers and unlock business features.

One-time payment

R50

✓ Verified badge

✓ More visibility

✓ Accept bookings

✓ Analytics

✓ Promotions

✓ Flash Deals

No monthly fees.

No hidden costs.

Pay once.

If they never verify

Their business can still exist on FOMO, but with some limitations:

No verified badge

Lower search ranking

Cannot receive bookings through FOMO

No analytics

No Flash Deals

No promotions

Limited profile customization

This creates a natural incentive to verify without making the platform feel pay-to-play.

Extend the same model across FOMO

The beauty of this approach is that it can be applied consistently across your ecosystem:

Prep Rooms — R50 once-off verification

Nightlife Venues — R50 once-off verification

Restaurants — R50 once-off verification

Creators (DJ, Artist, Photographer, MC, Promoter) — R50 once-off verification

Event Organizers — R50 once-off verification

A single, transparent rule—create for free, verify once—is easy for businesses to understand and scales well as FOMO grows.
