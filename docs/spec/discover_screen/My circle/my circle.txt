FOMO — My Circle
Full Updated Production Plan (Apple-tier Premium)
This redesign shifts My Circle away from looking like a social media feed and turns it into a premium social hub for your nightlife community. It focuses on people first, with elegant spacing, immersive visuals, and WhatsApp/Facebook-inspired stories adapted for FOMO.

My Circle helps users:
Stay connected with friends
Discover new people nearby
Meet people at venues and events
View stories from friends and creators
Coordinate nights out

Premium Motion
The screen should feel alive with subtle, polished animations:
Story rings animate softly when new content arrives.
Live badges pulse gently.
Cards lift slightly on touch with haptic feedback.
Story transitions use immersive full-screen animations.
Activity updates fade in smoothly without disrupting scrolling.

Design Principles
Apple-inspired spacing and typography.
Large, high-quality avatars as the visual focus.
Minimal use of neon—reserved for status accents only.
Glassmorphism used sparingly for overlays and action buttons.
Consistent rounded corners across cards and controls.
Clear visual hierarchy with generous whitespace.

One additional refinement I recommend is moving the Friends / Followers / Following counters out of the main content area and onto the user's profile screen. That keeps My Circle focused on what users came to do—see what their friends are doing and discover people—while giving the stories row much more visual prominence, resulting in a cleaner, more premium first impression.
