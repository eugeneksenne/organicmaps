# FOMO Channels — Premium Global Nightlife Network

## Design Philosophy

The new Channels experience is no longer a traditional social feed.

It becomes the **live operating system for nightlife.**

The goal is to answer three questions within five seconds:

* What's happening right now?
* Where should I go?
* Why should I go there?

Everything else is secondary.

---

# Design System

* Dark-first interface
* Large cinematic photography
* Large typography
* Plenty of whitespace
* Glassmorphism only where it adds focus
* Rounded 28–32 px corners
* Minimal icons
* Purple accent reserved for key actions
* Red reserved exclusively for live/urgent states
* Information revealed progressively rather than all at once

## Why this is a stronger direction

This redesign removes the "dashboard" feeling and replaces it with an immersive, premium experience. Rather than showing every metric at once, it tells a story: you arrive, understand the city's nightlife in seconds, discover where to go, and then dive deeper if you choose. It aligns much better with the Apple-tier, consumer-first design language you've established across FOMO's Discover, Events, Smart Places, and Map screens.
