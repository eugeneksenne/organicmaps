# FOMO — Dynamic Hero System

## The Living Pulse of the City

---

# Product Vision

The **Dynamic Hero System** is the emotional heartbeat of FOMO.

It is the first experience users encounter on the Discover screen and serves as the city's live showcase.

Rather than displaying static banners, the Hero System presents an intelligent carousel of real-time experiences, helping users instantly answer one question:

> **"What's worth checking out right now?"**

Every Hero card tells one story, has one purpose, and leads to one meaningful action.

The Hero continuously adapts using live city intelligence to ensure every visit feels fresh, relevant, and connected to what's happening around the user.

---

# Core Product Goal

Within the first **2 seconds**, users should feel:

* Curiosity
* Excitement
* Urgency
* Discovery

The Hero should create the feeling that the city is alive and invite users to become part of it.

# Hero Carousel

The Hero is a horizontally swipeable carousel.

Each card represents one important story happening in or around the city.

Only relevant cards are displayed.

---

# Carousel Behaviour

## Automatic Rotation

The carousel automatically advances every **6–8 seconds**.

Behaviour:

* Smooth left-slide animation
* Infinite looping
* Progress indicator for the active card
* Rotation pauses while the user is interacting
* Automatically resumes after a short period of inactivity

---

## Manual Navigation

Users can:

* Swipe left
* Swipe right
* Tap pagination indicators
* Tap any Hero card

Manual interaction always takes priority over automatic movement.

# Dynamic Hero Intelligence

The Hero System adapts using live contextual signals.

Signals include:

* Time of day
* User location
* Nearby venues
* Live events
* Weather conditions

# Motion System

Motion should feel cinematic and effortless.

Animations include:

* Automatic horizontal slide
* Manual swipe gestures
* Background parallax
* Ambient lighting movement
* Smooth CTA transitions
* Animated pagination indicators
* Gentle card elevation

Avoid:

* Aggressive motion
* Fast transitions
* Distracting effects

---

# Visual Language

The Hero should feel like a premium city magazine.

Use:

* Full-width immersive photography
* Short looping background videos
* Dark cinematic gradients
* Glassmorphism overlays
* Electric purple accents
* Warm amber highlights
* Live status badges
* Premium typography
* Soft depth and shadows

---

# Personalization

The Hero System becomes smarter over time.

It learns from:

* Preferred venue categories
* Frequently visited areas
* Favourite venue types
* Time-of-day usage patterns
* Previously viewed venues

The goal is to improve relevance without limiting discovery.

---

# Safety & Trust

The Hero must never:

* Display fabricated popularity.
* Invent attendance numbers.
* Misrepresent venue activity.
* Promote unsafe or restricted locations.

Sponsored content must always be clearly labeled.

Authenticity always takes priority over engagement.

---

# Performance & Scalability

Built to support millions of users.

* Lazy-loaded Hero media
* Progressive image and video loading
* Intelligent content caching
* GPU-accelerated animations
* Optimized 60 FPS scrolling
* Adaptive media quality based on network conditions
* Background content prefetching for seamless transitions

---

# Monetization

The Hero Carousel supports premium placements while preserving trust.

Supported placements:

* Sponsored Spotlights
* Venue Takeovers
* Brand Campaigns
* Tourism Promotions
* Seasonal City Campaigns

Sponsored cards are limited in frequency and never overshadow organic city discovery.

---

# Product Principles

* **Discovery first:** Help users quickly decide where to go.
* **One story per card:** Every Hero focuses on a single experience.
* **City-first intelligence:** Reflect the real rhythm of the city throughout the day.
* **Authenticity by design:** Use real, verifiable information instead of artificial hype.
* **Premium simplicity:** Keep the experience immersive, elegant, and uncluttered.
* **Nightlife at the core:** Every card should reinforce FOMO as the platform for discovering nightlife and city experiences.

---

# Final Positioning

The **Dynamic Hero System** is the living front page of FOMO. It blends real-time city intelligence, featured venues, major events, weather-aware recommendations, the city's reigning Mayor, official campaigns, and premium sponsorships into a beautifully curated carousel that evolves throughout the day. More than just a banner, it becomes the emotional gateway to the app—welcoming users into the rhythm of their city and helping them answer one simple question every time they open FOMO:

> **"Where should I go next?"**
