
# FOMO — Mayor System

## The Face of the City's Nightlife

---

# Product Vision

Every city has a face.

Every nightlife scene has a leader.

On FOMO, that leader is known as the **Mayor**.

The Mayor is the individual who is currently making the greatest positive impact on their city's nightlife culture through authentic participation, influence, and contribution.

There is only:

* 👑 One Mayor per city
* 🌆 One active reign
* 🏆 One nightlife leader

The title cannot be:

* Purchased
* Voted for
* Assigned manually

It can only be earned.

---

# Core Philosophy

Everything in the Mayor system answers one question:

> **Who is shaping nightlife in this city right now?**

If a signal doesn't help answer that question, it should not influence Mayor rankings.

---

# Product Principles

The Mayor system is built on five principles:

* Authenticity over popularity
* Real-world experiences over online activity
* Contribution over consumption
* Consistency over viral moments
* Merit over monetization

---

# Eligibility

To enter the Mayor rankings, users must satisfy baseline trust requirements.

Examples:

* Verified account
* Eligible city
* Authentic nightlife participation
* Minimum nightlife activity
* Good standing within the community

Only eligible users compete.

---

# The Nightlife Influence Engine

The Mayor Score is calculated from four weighted pillars.

---

## 🌙 1. Nightlife Presence

**Highest weighting**

Measures genuine participation in the city's nightlife.

Signals include:

* Verified venue visits
* Event attendance
* Venue dwell time
* Club Lobby participation
* Flash Drop participation
* Verified movement between nightlife locations

This reflects being present where nightlife happens.

---

## 🎥 2. Nightlife Contribution

Measures what the user contributes back to the community.

Examples:

* Moments
* Photos
* Videos
* Venue reviews
* Live broadcasts (future)

Quality always outweighs quantity.

---

## 🌊 3. Nightlife Influence

Measures whether the community values those contributions.

Signals include:

* Ripple Score
* Shares
* Saves
* Meaningful comments
* Watch time
* Venue engagement

Simple likes carry little weight.

---

## 📈 4. Consistency

Nightlife leadership must be maintained.

Signals include:

* Regular activity
* Sustained contribution
* Continued presence
* Long-term influence

The Mayor isn't crowned for one great weekend.

---

# Signals That Never Count

The following contribute **zero** Mayor points:

* Chats
* Friend requests
* Profile views
* Messaging
* Generic Discover usage
* AI interactions
* Artificial engagement
* Purchased promotion
* Prep-only activity
* Non-nightlife content

This protects the integrity of the title.

---

# City Leaderboards

Each city has an independent leaderboard.

Visible:

* Top 10

For everyone else:

* Top 25%
* Top 50%
* Top 100

This keeps the Top 10 genuinely prestigious.

---

# Becoming Mayor

The crown is never transferred instantly.

Progression:

```text
Top 10

↓

Top 3

↓

#2

↓

Challenge Current Mayor

↓

Integrity Validation

↓

👑 Crowned Mayor
```

Before a takeover, the system verifies:

* Anti-fraud
* Location integrity
* Protection margin
* Score stability

Only then is the crown awarded.

---

# Crown Protection

To prevent rapid crown changes:

Requirements:

* Challenger exceeds the Mayor's score
* Configurable lead margin
* Lead maintained during a validation window
* Integrity checks passed

This creates stable reigns.

---

# Losing the Crown

A Mayor loses the title only if:

* Another user legitimately surpasses them
* Their influence naturally declines
* Fraud is confirmed

Nothing else.

---

# One Living Mayor

There are no:

* Weekly Mayors
* Monthly Mayors
* Seasonal Mayors

There is only one current Mayor.

A reign ends only when someone earns the crown.

---

# Mayor Legacy

Every reign becomes permanent history.

Profiles display:

* Current Mayor status
* Longest reign
* Total crowns earned
* Cities ruled
* Total days as Mayor
* Successful defenses
* Crown takeovers
* Highest influence score
* First crown date
* Latest crown date

This becomes one of the highest achievements on FOMO.

---

# Hall of Fame

Every city maintains a permanent Hall of Fame.

Including:

* Current Mayor
* Longest Reign
* Most Crowns
* Biggest Takeover
* Highest Influence Score
* City Legend
* First Mayor

This preserves the city's nightlife history.

The Mayor system relies on production-grade protections.

Including:

* GPS spoof detection
* City farming detection
* Engagement ring detection
* Trust scoring
* Behavioural analysis
* Rate limiting
* Event sourcing
* Incremental score recalculation
* Redis caching
* Background workers
* Appeals process

Trust is more important than engagement.

# Design Language

The Mayor should feel:

* Prestigious
* Aspirational
* Authentic
* Local
* Cinematic
* Exclusive
* Respected

Never:

* Gamified
* Pay-to-win
* Popularity-driven
* Flashy for the sake of attention

The crown should feel earned.

---

# Final Positioning

The **Mayor** is FOMO's flagship identity system. Every city has one living nightlife leader whose reputation is built on authentic participation, meaningful contribution, and lasting influence within the local nightlife scene. By weaving the Mayor naturally into Discover, Maps, Venue Profiles, Club Lobbies, and the launch experience, FOMO gives every city a recognizable face while celebrating the people who genuinely shape its nightlife culture. The result is a prestigious, merit-based title that feels aspirational, trustworthy, and deeply connected to the real world—not just to activity inside the app.
