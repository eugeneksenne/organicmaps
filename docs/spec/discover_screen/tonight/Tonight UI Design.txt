FOMO Discover — Tonight Screen (V2)

Premium UI/UX Design Specification

This redesign transforms Tonight from a dashboard into the operating system for the user's night.

The screen should answer four questions within 3 seconds:

Am I safe?

What's happening tonight?

What are my plans?

What's my next move?

The experience should feel like a blend of Apple Wallet, Uber Live Activities, Netflix, and Tesla—clean, cinematic, and highly interactive.

Product Goals

The Tonight screen is where users:

View and manage NightGuard

Create nightlife plans

Join invitations

Coordinate with friends

Track live progress

Receive AI recommendations

Stay safe

Manage everything for tonight

It is not a settings screen.

It is not a dashboard.

It is the command center for the night.

Premium UX Principles

One primary action per section.

Never show more than one AI suggestion at a time.

Surface the "Create Plan" action everywhere it makes sense.

NightGuard should always remain one tap away.

Users should understand their entire night in less than 5 seconds.

Every card should have a clear purpose—no decorative widgets or redundant metrics.

The interface should feel calm and effortless, using motion and spacing rather than visual clutter to communicate importance.

This structure makes the Tonight screen feel less like a feature list and more like a luxury, real-time nightlife companion that users will naturally return to before, during, and after every night out.
