# FOMO — Explore the City

## Billion-Dollar Discover Experience (MVP)

---

# Product Vision

**Explore the City** is FOMO's intelligent venue discovery experience.

It helps users discover **the best places around them** based on the city's rhythm, their location, and what's happening right now.

Unlike Google Maps, Explore is not a business directory.

Unlike Instagram, it isn't a social feed.

Unlike TripAdvisor, it isn't a review platform.

Explore exists to answer one simple question:

> **"Where should I go right now?"**

# Product Principles

* **Nightlife-first:** Every place should support social experiences and going out.
* **Discover worlds, not databases:** Users browse experiences, not exhaustive business categories.
* **Adapt to the city's rhythm:** Content changes naturally with the time of day.
* **Authenticity over hype:** Use real venue data instead of artificial popularity.
* **Fast decision-making:** Every element should help users answer, *"Where should I go right now?"*

---

# Final Positioning

The **Explore the City** screen is the heartbeat of FOMO. It combines a dynamic city-aware hero, intuitive Discovery Worlds, and curated venue recommendations into a seamless experience that evolves with the day. Rather than overwhelming users with endless lists or categories, it presents the city's best social destinations in context—making it effortless to discover, decide, and head out. This focused approach establishes FOMO as a premium nightlife discovery platform while providing a scalable foundation for future personalization, AI, and global expansion.
