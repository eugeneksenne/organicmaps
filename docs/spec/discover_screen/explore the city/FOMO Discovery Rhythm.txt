## FOMO Discovery Rhythm

Don't think of Explore as static.

Think of it as **following the city's rhythm**.

The order of discovery worlds changes automatically based on:

* Time of day
* Day of week
* Public holidays
* Local events
* User location
* Seasonal trends (future)

# Dynamic Card Ranking

Not only should the **sections** reorder, but the **venues inside them** should as well.

For example:

At **9 AM**:

```text
🍽 Food

Open for Breakfast

☕ Father Coffee

🥐 Tashas

🥯 Seattle Coffee
```

At **8 PM**:

```text
🍽 Food

Dinner Tonight

🍣 Marble

🍝 Saint

🥩 The Grillhouse
```

At **2 AM**:

```text
🍽 Food

Still Open

🍔 McDonald's
```

The same category feels completely different depending on the time.

# The FOMO Clock

This could become one of the app's signature systems.

Every part of Discover adapts to the city's daily rhythm:

* The order of discovery worlds changes.
* The hero messaging changes.
* Category artwork shifts from daylight to nightlife.
* Venue rankings update based on relevance.
* Search suggestions become time-aware (e.g., "Breakfast near me" in the morning, "Cocktail bars" in the evening).
* Heatmaps emphasize lunch districts by day and nightlife districts by night.

Users may not consciously notice these changes, but they'll consistently feel that **FOMO always knows what they're looking for**. That's the hallmark of a premium product: the interface quietly adapts to the user's context instead of asking them to do the work.
