# FOMO Global Venue Intelligence Platform

## Production Architecture Specification v1.0

This is the **upstream permanent venue intelligence system**. I am keeping its role distinct from the **FOMO Global Venue Pack Creation Intelligence Platform** and the **Country Pack Installation Strategy**.

The uploaded architecture defines Global Venue Intelligence as the system that creates the permanent venue universe, with the Canonical Venue Store as its authoritative source of truth. 

---

# 1. Vision

The **FOMO Global Venue Intelligence Platform** is the permanent data foundation of FOMO.

Its purpose is to discover, understand, identify, verify, normalize, classify and continuously maintain the world's supported physical venues.

It creates the authoritative venue universe that downstream FOMO systems consume.

```text
GLOBAL VENUE INTELLIGENCE
          ↓
CANONICAL VENUE STORE
          ↓
GLOBAL VENUE PACK CREATION
          ↓
COUNTRY PACKS
          ↓
DEVICE VENUE REPLICAS
          ↓
FOMO MAP ENGINE
```

The Canonical Venue Store is the source of truth. Country Packs are distribution snapshots, and device databases are local runtime replicas. 

---

# 2. Mission

> **Create the most reliable permanent venue intelligence layer for FOMO's global social and lifestyle ecosystem.**

Every production venue should be:

* uniquely identified
* geographically anchored
* normalized
* deduplicated
* classified
* verified
* quality-scored
* permanently identifiable
* geographically indexed
* continuously maintainable
* ready for downstream packaging

The platform is concerned with **venue truth**, not the user-facing FOMO experience.

# 68. Non-Negotiable Rules

### Rule 1

**Every permanent venue has exactly one FOMO Venue ID.**

### Rule 2

**The FOMO Venue ID never changes.**

### Rule 3

**FOMO Venue IDs are never recycled.**

### Rule 4

**Provider IDs remain reference fields only.**

### Rule 5

**Canonical Venue Store is the authoritative source of truth.**

### Rule 6

**Country Packs are distribution snapshots.**

### Rule 7

**Device databases are local replicas.**

### Rule 8

**Pack membership is independent of venue identity.**

### Rule 9

**Events never create duplicate permanent venues.**

### Rule 10

**Realtime activity never becomes static Country Pack data.**

### Rule 11

**Media references are stored in venue records, while media assets live in object storage.**

### Rule 12

**All venue changes are keyed by FOMO Venue ID.**

### Rule 13

**Temporary closure does not automatically destroy venue identity.**

### Rule 14

**Duplicate branches must receive distinct FOMO Venue IDs.**

### Rule 15

**External provider schemas never become the FOMO canonical schema.**

These rules are directly supported by the uploaded Global Venue and geographic architecture.

## The key architectural distinction

The three venue systems now have very clean boundaries:

**FOMO Global Venue Intelligence Platform**

> **Creates and maintains the truth about venues.**

**FOMO Global Venue Pack Creation Intelligence Platform**

> **Turns that truth into optimized, versioned Country Packs.**

**FOMO Final Country Pack Installation Strategy**

> **Moves those packs onto devices and keeps them current.**

And finally:

**FOMO Offline Map Engine**

> **Combines the installed venue intelligence with Organic Maps to power the actual FOMO map.**

That separation is strongly supported by the uploaded architecture, particularly its distinction between the Canonical Venue Store, Country Packs, device replicas, and Organic Maps.
