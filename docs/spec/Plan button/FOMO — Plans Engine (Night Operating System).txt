# FOMO Planning Philosophy (Night Operating System)

## Vision

**Plan together. Move together. Experience together. Remember forever.**

Planning in FOMO is not a feature or a checklist.

It is a **living operating system** that manages an entire night out—from the first idea to the final memory.

Users should never feel like they are opening a traditional planner.

Instead, they should feel like they are entering a **private command center** that intelligently coordinates people, places, timing, transport, safety, spending, reservations, and memories in one seamless experience. This aligns with the Plans Engine vision and lifecycle described in your architecture.  

---

# Core Philosophy

> **Discovery inspires. Planning coordinates. Experiences unfold. Memories last forever.**

Every experience inside FOMO naturally progresses through four stages.

```
Discover
      ↓
Plan
      ↓
Experience
      ↓
Remember
```

The interface adapts automatically as users move through each stage.

---

# Stage 1 — Discover

## Purpose

Help users discover experiences worth sharing.

Users browse:

* Venues
* Events
* Restaurants
* Festivals
* Creators
* Friends
* Channels
* AI recommendations

At this stage users are exploring.

They haven't decided where to go yet.

Planning should never interrupt discovery.

---

## Discovery Screens

* Discover
* Feed
* Stories
* Map
* Search
* Creator Profiles
* Venue Listings

These screens exist to inspire curiosity.

---

# Stage 2 — Plan

## Purpose

Transform an interesting discovery into a coordinated night.

Planning begins only after users decide:

> "I want this to be part of my night."

At that moment, the Plans Engine activates.

---

# The Plan Wizard

The Plan button never creates a plan immediately.

Instead, it launches a lightweight **Plan Wizard** that guides users into the right planning experience.

## Step 1 — Choose Your Night

Three planning modes are available.

### Solo Plan

Perfect for independent nightlife.

Best for:

* Exploring new venues
* Solo dinners
* Club hopping
* Business travel
* Personal adventures

The Plans Engine automatically prepares:

* AI itinerary
* Route optimisation
* Budget
* Calendar
* NightGuard
* Personal memories

These capabilities are part of the Solo Plans Engine. 

---

### Duo Plan

Perfect for:

* Dates
* Couples
* Best friends

The wizard asks:

Who are you planning with?

Once accepted, the engine creates:

* Shared timeline
* Shared chat
* Shared budget
* Calendar sync
* Ride coordination
* Optional live location

These are the collaboration features defined for Duo plans. 

---

### Group Plan

Perfect for:

* Birthdays
* Friend groups
* Club crews
* Festivals
* Work socials

The wizard allows users to:

* Add members
* Select a host
* Invite everyone
* Define a budget
* Pick a start time

The engine then enables:

* Live attendance
* Polls
* Shared expenses
* Temporary group chat
* Shared album
* AI coordination

Matching the Group Plans Engine capabilities. 

---

The plan becomes alive.

It continuously updates throughout the night.

---

* Venue changes
* Ride progress
* Check-ins

---

## Live Map

Displays:

* ETA
* Ride progress

Helping everyone stay coordinated.

# The Tonight Hub

The **Tonight** section is the permanent home of the Plans Engine.

From here users can:

* Create a Solo, Duo, or Group plan
* Continue active plans
* Manage invitations
* Browse templates
* Explore public plans
* View planning history

It serves as the user's command center for every night out.

---

# Plan Button Philosophy

The **Plan** button is **not** a universal call-to-action.

It is a contextual gateway into FOMO's Night Operating System.

It appears only when the user has enough information to confidently decide:

> **"I want this to be part of my night."**

---

# Where the Plan Button Appears

Planning entry points are reserved for high-intent moments:

* Venue Details
* Event Details
* Restaurant Details
* Venue preview cards on the Map
* Search results
* Chat conversations
* User profiles (**Plan Together**)

---

# Where It Does Not Appear

Planning is intentionally absent from low-intent discovery surfaces:

* Feed
* Stories
* General Discover cards
* Creator posts
* Camera
* Memories

These screens are designed to inspire, not to initiate planning.

---

# Active Plan Experience

Once a plan exists, the **Plan** button is replaced by a persistent **Tonight** pill above the bottom navigation.

The pill reflects the current state of the night:

* Draft
* Planning
* Waiting for replies
* Countdown
* Active
* Ride in progress
* Completed

Tapping it instantly returns the user to the live Plans Engine, ensuring that planning remains a continuous experience rather than a repeated action.

---

# Final Design Rule

> **The Plan button is not a feature—it is the gateway into FOMO's Night Operating System. Discovery surfaces inspire exploration, decision surfaces launch the Plan Wizard, and once a plan is created, the Tonight hub becomes the user's living command center. Every Solo, Duo, and Group plan evolves before, during, and after the night through AI, collaboration, realtime coordination, and memories, making FOMO the operating system for nightlife rather than just another discovery app.**
