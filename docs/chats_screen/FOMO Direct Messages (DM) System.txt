The DM (Direct Messages) screen is where users will spend the majority of their time, so it needs to be the most polished screen in FOMO. It should match the responsiveness of WhatsApp, the animations of iMessage, the media experience of Telegram, and the social integration of FOMO.
I would design the DM system as follows.

Security
For direct messages:
End-to-end encryption for message content.
Separate encryption keys for media.
Device verification for new logins.
Rate limiting to prevent spam.
Attachment malware scanning before distribution.
Abuse reporting and user blocking.
Automatic key rotation for long-term security.

Performance Targets
Conversation open: <100 ms
Send acknowledgement: <30 ms
Typing indicator: <50 ms
Media preview: instant from cache
Image upload: background with progressive progress
Voice note playback: <50 ms startup
Smooth scrolling at 60–120 FPS depending on device capability.

FOMO-Specific Integrations
This is where FOMO can differentiate itself from traditional messengers.
The DM should allow users to:
Share venues directly from the Map.
Share events from Discover with rich previews.
Share Moments from the Feed.
Share routes from the FOMO Route Engine.
Start Buddy Pair, Safety Check, or Walk Me Home directly from the conversation.
Open a shared venue into its Club Lobby without leaving the messaging context.
Final Production Assessment
A DM system built with these capabilities would be comparable to the leading messaging platforms in reliability and polish, while providing unique integrations with FOMO's venue, event, routing, and safety ecosystem. Rather than being "WhatsApp inside FOMO," it becomes the communication layer that ties together every core feature of the app.
