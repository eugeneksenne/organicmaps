Purpose

The Stories section answers one simple question:

"What's happening with my friends and the people I follow today?"

This is a personal social layer designed to encourage conversations.

Billion-Dollar Product Principle

The Chats Stories section should strengthen personal relationships, not compete with Explore.

Chats answers: "What are my friends sharing?"

Explore answers: "What's happening in the city?"

Club Lobby answers: "What's happening at this venue?"

Keeping these responsibilities separate gives FOMO a much clearer information architecture, reduces clutter, and makes each part of the app easier to understand and scale.
