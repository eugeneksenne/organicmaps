I think the Calls section should become one of FOMO's strongest differentiators. Rather than trying to compete feature-for-feature with Zoom or Discord, it should focus on high-quality social communication that integrates with FOMO's nightlife ecosystem.
The system should be built around a single calling engine that powers one-to-one calls, group calls, and voice/video experiences across the app.
FOMO Calls System
Architecture
One calling engine powers:
Direct Voice Calls
Direct Video Calls
Group Voice Calls
Group Video Calls
Voice calls from DMs
Calls from Groups
Calls from Club Lobbies (approved members only)
Buddy Pair emergency voice calls
NightGuard emergency calls
Every call shares the same signaling, reconnect, permissions, notifications, encryption, and media pipeline.

NightGuard Integration
One of FOMO's unique strengths.
During a Buddy Pair or Walk Me Home session, the call interface can display:
Shared live location
Distance between participants
Estimated arrival time
Battery level
Safety status
This provides context without interrupting the conversation.

Club Lobby Calls
Rather than creating public voice channels, Club Lobbies can support scheduled group calls for approved organizers and participants.
Examples:
Event organizer briefings
VIP planning
Staff coordination
This keeps the feature aligned with FOMO's venue-first design instead of becoming a general-purpose voice platform.

Security
Production-grade protections:
End-to-end encryption for call media.
Secure WebRTC signaling.
TURN/STUN fallback for NAT traversal.
Authenticated call invitations.
Spam and call-rate limiting.
Device verification.
Abuse reporting and blocking.
Secure ICE candidate validation.

Performance Targets
Outgoing call screen: <100 ms
Ring initiation: <300 ms
Voice call connection: <1.5 s
Video call connection: <2 s
Camera switch: <250 ms
Reconnection after brief network loss: <3 s
Voice latency: <150 ms
Adaptive media quality with minimal interruptions.
Battery-efficient background operation.
Production Assessment
This design gives FOMO a communication system comparable to leading messaging platforms while remaining focused on its core identity. The strongest differentiators are the seamless integration with DMs, Groups, Club Lobbies, and NightGuard, allowing calls to support real-world social experiences instead of existing as an isolated feature.
